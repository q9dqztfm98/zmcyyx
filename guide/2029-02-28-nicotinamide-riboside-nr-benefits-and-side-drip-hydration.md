What Is NAD+ and Why Is It Important?

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) is a coenzyme found in all living cells. It plays a central role in cellular energy metabolism, acting as a cofactor in energy transformation—especially mitochondrial ATP production. It also regulates DNA repair, chromatin remodeling, senescence, and immune function. [1]

However, NAD+ naturally declines with age, obesity, hypertension, and other metabolic stresses—contributing to diminished cellular resilience and increased disease risk [2]

What Is NAD+ IV Therapy?

NAD+ IV therapy infuses NAD+ directly into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive tract for maximum bioavailability. It’s marketed for delivering boosts in energy, mental clarity, recovery, anti-aging effects, and cellular detox.

What Is Niagen (NR) IV Therapy?

Niagen® is a branded form of nicotinamide riboside (NR), a precursor to NAD+. Niagen IV therapy delivers NR directly into the bloodstream, allowing cells to convert it into NAD+ over time.

This method may offer gentler, better tolerated, and more efficient NAD+ increases with fewer side effects like headache or nausea.

Benefits often cited include improved metabolic health, energy, recovery, inflammation reduction, cognitive clarity, and cellular rejuvenation.
