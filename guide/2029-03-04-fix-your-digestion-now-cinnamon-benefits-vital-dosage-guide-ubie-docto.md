Cinnamon may support digestion by slowing the breakdown of starches in the gut, which can help regulate blood sugar spikes after meals and ease bloating or discomfort. However, official recommended daily intake amounts for digestive benefits have not been clearly established in current medical references. Cinnamon has been studied for its potential effects on digestion, particularly how it might help slow down the breakdown of starches. According to research, cinnamon can inhibit certain enzymes that are responsible for breaking down starches, which may help support digestion by making the process slower and more controlled. This can be beneficial in managing how quickly sugar enters the bloodstream after eating starch-rich foods.
