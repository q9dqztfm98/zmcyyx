How do medications from BlueChew and Roman fight ED?

Both BlueChew and Roman offer medications called PDE5 inhibitors.5 This is the class of medications that includes Viagra and Cialis. To understand how they work, it helps to know a bit about the mechanics of an erection and the common physiological symptoms of ED.6

When you get aroused, your body releases cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), which relaxes the smooth muscle tissue in the penis and allows it to engorge with blood. That added blood expands the penis, resulting in an erection.

But what if your erection didn’t go away after climax? That would certainly have posed a problem for our ancestors trying to outrun bears. So, humans developed an off-switch called phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE5).

PDE5 is an enzyme that causes the breakdown of cGMP, and as your body runs low on cGMP, the muscles in the penis constrict, blood flow reduces, and your erection goes away. With many ED cases, there’s either too little cGMP produced upon arousal or PDE5 is too abundant or being released too early (before climax). PDE5 inhibitors prevent the enzymatic activity of PDE5, allowing cGMP to continue building up and doing its job: maintaining relaxed smooth muscles and the blood flow needed for a healthy erection.7

This is why the common misconceptions that Viagra will directly cause an erection or result in hours-long erections are incorrect.8 Rather, Viagra and other PDE5 inhibitors allow you to respond to arousal with an erection and to keep that erection long enough to enjoy penetrative sex to climax. They don’t magically make you hard out of nowhere. After climax, your body’s PDE5 production picks up dramatically, typically with more intensity than PDE5 inhibitors are designed to counteract. That way, you won’t have an erection for hours after intercourse (if you do, this is a reason to call your doctor).

BlueChew Gold takes things a couple of steps further than this duo, starting with a base of sildenafil and tadalafil, then adding the apomorphine seen in Rugiet Ready and a fourth ingredient: oxytocin.

Let’s look at how these two additional medications work so we can better understand how BlueChew Gold meaningfully differentiates itself from other multitherapies on the market, including Ro Sparks:

Apomorphine

Apomorphine is a dopamine receptor agonist, so it essentially tricks the brain into believing it’s getting more dopamine than it is. This mechanism has a handful of compelling downstream effects, one of which is improved erectile performance.3 Various individual studies have illustrated efficacy and good tolerance (save for some nausea), with one study showing efficacy across the 2mg-4mg range seen in BlueChew Gold.20

Oxytocin

At this time, ED research on oxytocin research is mostly limited to animal studies, but it has been shown to help in cases of anxiety and depression.17 Considering that around 40% of ED cases are psychogenic in origin — based in mental rather than physical health — oxytocin could make a serious difference for many men.18 It’s not entirely clear how BlueChew should dose its oxytocin, as most studies into mental health and libido use nasal spray administration, which can get into the blood and potentially even cross the blood-brain barrier more readily than oral or sublingual administration.21

Combined with Gold’s sildenafil and tadalafil doses, these two ingredients round out a formula that can tackle ED from blood flow, neurochemical, and psychological sources. And when you consider that many ED pathologies can include all three contributing factors, it makes total sense to seek a medication that can tackle all of them.

One other really nice thing about BlueChew’s combination offerings is that they’re delivered as a chewable tablet that can also quickly dissolve under your tongue and can theoretically start working faster than drugs absorbed in the gut. Sparks is merely a sublingual tablet — no real option to chew it — and it takes much longer to dissolve than BlueChew.

Sildenafil is the active ingredient in Viagra, and it’s therapeutically identical to Viagra in its generic form. Either one will take around an hour to kick in and should last for 4-6 hours.9 Both have the same side effect profile, too, with some cardiovascular risks and the potential for visual disturbances and muscle pain. But generic sildenafil is a lot less expensive than branded Viagra That being said, several competitors offer traditional sildenafil for a lower price than Roman.

Tadalafil

You can take tadalafil either as needed or as a daily medication. When taken as needed, it will last in the body for anywhere from 12 to 36 hours, depending on things like dosage, your metabolism, and whether you took it on an empty stomach.10 Like sildenafil, it can take up to an hour to start working.

When taken daily, tadalafil builds up in your system and is always there if you happen to need it.11 But if you aren’t especially sexually active, taking a daily medication might not be your best course of action.

Again, we find that Roman’s tadalafil tablets are vastly overpriced, with doses for generic tadalafil landing between $8 and $11 each. Compared to Hims’ $1.07 per dose for its tablets or the $1.30 per dose for its chewable tadalafil, Roman’s price is pretty hard to swallow.

Alternatives to BlueChew and Roman

There are a handful of trustworthy telehealth companies that can provide prescriptions for ED medications, and we’ve spent considerable time evaluating each of them. We’ve even mentioned some of them in this guide. Here’s a quick snapshot, including some options better than either BlueChew or Roman:

Hims

Hims resembles Roman in that it’s a large company with a wide range of telehealth services, including hair loss treatments, mental health care, and skin care. It offers some of the best prices around on tadalafil, but accessing those prices requires a specific combination of a subscription program and dose combination that isn’t going to be ideal for all men.

Hims offers what it calls Hard Mints and Sex Chews in an attempt to capture some of the audience to whom BlueChew’s chewables appeal. However, drug availability, dosing options, and pricing are obscured on the site, with limited public-facing information. We hope Hims will be more forthcoming about these price structures and dose combinations in the future.

One important area of consideration for Hims is its chewable vardenafil — an alternative to sildenafil for men who experience certain side effects from the latter that the former typically doesn’t cause.12 14 Hims’ vardenafil is exceptionally well-priced, and it’s not widely available through competitors.

Rocket Rx

Rocket Rx has a more limited ED med catalog than Hims, but it also offers better prices on sildenafil tablets than anyone else on the market, with a broad dose range and good customer service. The cost of its tadalafil is also very close to Hims’ best prices, and the company doesn’t require enormous shipping volumes to reach its lowest price points: buying one or two months’ worth of pills takes its prices down to extremely competitive levels. That allows more men to try ED meds without a big financial commitment.

Compared to BlueChew Gold and Ro Sparks, Rocket offers a similar combination of sildenafil and tadalafil with greater dose flexibility and better prices. That said, BlueChew Gold’s and Ro Sparks’ doses strike a balance between a medium dose of sildenafil and a high dose of tadalafil that’s currently missing from Rocket’s catalog. It’d likely be smarter for men new to combination treatment to start with Rocket Rx, but they might have to migrate over to BlueChew at a certain point in their titration journey.

Rugiet

Rugiet offers various combination therapies to tackle ED, hair loss, sleep, and more. Its core product is Rugiet Ready, lozenges that contain a mixture of sildenafil, tadalafil, and apomorphine, typically in high doses. Working with your assigned doctor, you can customize your combination of drugs and doses, but the compounding process for these lozenges is where a lot of the cost comes from. If you want just one or two meds in your lozenges with low doses (where we recommend starting), you’ll likely be overpaying for them.

Promescent

Promescent started out as a climax delay spray, and the company grew to offer delay wipes, sex toys, and several other sexual wellness products. Very recently, the company launched a prescription ED system with competitive prices. But the program hasn’t been around long enough to determine whether the medical support you get while on these drugs is as robust as that offered by BlueChew, Roman, and Hims.

BlueChew and Roman FAQ

Do BlueChew and Roman offer the same ED drugs?

Roman offers only sildenafil and tadalafil, while BlueChew Gold contains sildenafil, tadalafil, apomorphine, and oxytocin. Roman offers one product that combines the two into Ro Sparks, but it comes in only a single dose increment, compared to three options for BlueChew Gold.

Which is cheaper, BlueChew or Roman?

Many of Roman’s monotherapies are less expensive than BlueChew Gold, but Gold contains four ED-fighting ingredients, not just one. So, it’s not an apples-to-apples comparison. Roman’s monotherapy prices are also higher than those of other competitors, like Hims or Rocket Rx.

Can BlueChew or Roman cure ED?

There is no cure for ED, in the sense that no drug can make it go away and never come back. ED medications are an effective treatment because they allow you to achieve and maintain an erection, but their effects wear off after 4-36 hours, depending on the medication.

Can I do BlueChew and Roman at the same time?

You could theoretically get some products from each company, but you wouldn’t want to double-dose or mix and match medications without strict oversight from your physician.
