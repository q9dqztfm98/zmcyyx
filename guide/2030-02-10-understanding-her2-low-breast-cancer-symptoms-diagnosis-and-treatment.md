Prevention of HER2-Low Breast Cancer

[Understanding HER](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/6717439530.html)

There’s no guaranteed way to prevent HER2-low breast cancer. But certain lifestyle habits can lower a person’s chance of developing the disease.

[22]

Eat a healthy diet that includes lots of fresh veggies and fruits.

Exercise regularly.

Maintain a healthy weight.

Don’t drink alcohol, or limit the amount you consume.

Don’t smoke or use tobacco products.

Breastfeed if you're able to after childbirth.

Avoid or limit hormone therapy.

Related Conditions

Some conditions closely related to HER2-low breast cancer include:

HER2-positive breast cancer This type of breast cancer is characterized by too many HER2 proteins on breast cancer cells.

HER-2 negative breast cancer The breast cancer cells don’t have the HER2 protein.

Triple-negative breast cancer Triple-negative breast cancers don’t have estrogen receptors, progesterone receptors, or HER2.

Metastatic breast cancer Metastatic breast cancer has spread to another part of the body.

Inflammatory breast cancer (IBC) IBC is a rare type of breast cancer that makes breasts appear red, tender, and swollen.

Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) With DCIS, some of the cells that line the milk ducts of the breast have changed to appear like cancer cells.
