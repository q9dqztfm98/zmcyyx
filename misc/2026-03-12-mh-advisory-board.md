# MH Advisory Board

The Men's Health Advisory Board is comprised of the world's leading experts in fitness, nutrition, weight loss, sex and relationships, and more. Widely recognized as some of the most experienced, accredited, and trustworthy professionals in their respective fields, they each contribute regular columns to MensHealth.com through which they distill the latest research and science, highlight common myths and misconceptions, and give readers practical advice to lead healthier lives.

Cardiology; John Elefteriades, MD

John Elefteriades, MD, is the William W.L. Glenn Professor of Surgery (Cardiac) and former Chief of Cardiothoracic Surgery at Yale University and Yale New-Haven Hospital. Dr. Elefteriades is among the most clinically active academic surgeons in the country. He is also a recognized authority in interventions for the failing left ventricle, including coronary artery bypass grafting, left ventricular aneurysmectomy, and artificial heart implantation. Dr. Elefteriades directs the Aortic Institute at Yale, one of the nation's largest facilities for treatment of the dilated thoracic aorta. He conducts laboratory research in new techniques of heart transplantation. Among his research projects, he is working to identify the genetic mutations responsible for thoracic aortic aneurysms. Dr. Elefteriades is the author of multiple educational books along with the medical ethics thriller Transplant.
