Just as significant as the changes to his health was the impact that challenge had on his social and working life. "The first event I went to where I didn't drink alcohol, I was a nervous wreck. However, as the month went on, he found that he pleasantly surprised himself with how he was able to exercise his willpower in social situations.

"I've actually really enjoyed doing it, and it's taught me so many lessons," he says. "My head is so much clearer, I'm back into a routine, because I think after summer of partying and drinking, I lost my mojo. I was in a bit of a dark place, my creativity had gone...
