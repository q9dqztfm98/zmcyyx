# Sam Heughan Posted a Shirtless Photo While Challenging Fans to Work Out

Scottish actor Sam Heughan has been staying busy while stuck at home by keeping up with his personal fitness, crushing a bodyweight workout and sharing some thirsty evidence of his pumped, perspiring pectorals on Instagram.

"These bodyweight workouts are kicking my but!!!" He wrote, tagging in his charitable health and wellness campaign My Peak Challenge, and encouraging his followers to try out the workout programs.

Heughan, who can be currently seen starring in Bloodshot opposite Vin Diesel, founded My Peak Challenge as a way for people to "live healthier, happier and more balanced lives while raising funds to change lives." The organization recently launched a free of charge 30-day social distancing challenge to encourage people to practice self-isolating and flatten the curve, while staying active.
