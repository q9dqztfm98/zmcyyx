# About The Edge by Hone Health

About The Edge

Personal Health Meets Proven Science

Optimized health starts with smarter content.

Health is personal. Your body’s unique chemistry—hormones, metabolic markers, and other key biomarkers—shapes how you feel, perform, and age. Understanding what’s happening under the surface is the first step to optimizing it.

That’s where we come in.

The Edge delivers science-backed, expert-vetted content designed to help you optimize your lifespan (how many years you live) and your healthspan (the number of them spent in good health).

Our stories are crafted by experienced journalists who’ve written for outlets including Men’s Health, Women’s Health, The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, SHAPE, Prevention, Health, Popular Science, Reader’s Digest, Yoga Journal, and more.

We dig into the latest research on topics like hormone health, longevity, and weight management, interviewing physicians and other licensed healthcare providers and experts to simplify complex health information without cutting corners.
