Peptide Sciences Alternatives: What to Look For

Before jumping into vendor recommendations, it’s worth understanding what separates reliable suppliers from risky ones.

A lot of buyers focused heavily on price in the past. After the Peptide Sciences shutdown, the conversation has shifted toward documentation and operational stability.

Here are the criteria that matter most.

Third-party COAs with verifiable lot numbers

A legitimate Certificate of Analysis should come from a third-party laboratory, not from the vendor itself. Even more importantly, it should include a lot number tied to a specific batch.

HPLC analysis separates the target peptide from impurities and measures purity levels.Mass spectrometry confirms that the molecule being tested is actually the compound being claimed.

Compliance posture

Vendors who responded quickly to recent regulatory pressure tend to operate more cautiously and sustainably.

Suppliers who removed GLP-1 compounds after the ITC exclusion orders demonstrated they were paying attention to the legal environment. Vendors ignoring those developments may not be around long.
