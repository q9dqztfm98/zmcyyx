What does ‘Natural’ mean?

Natural or natty is used to refer to someone who has not used PEDs (performance enhancing drugs), anabolic steroids or any products enlisted by the World Anti-Doping Agency.

These may include:

Steroids

Hormones

Certain Stimulants

Certain Fat Loss agents

When we label an athlete as ‘natural’ or ‘natty,’ we affirm that they refrain from utilizing any substances listed by the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA). This means that their physique was not achieved by the use of artificial enhancers that can stimulate muscle growth or increase strength.

This categorical exclusion covers a spectrum of potentially performance-altering agents, including steroids, hormones, certain stimulants, and specific fat loss agents.

For these athletes, the canvas of their physique is painted with the hues of disciplined training, meticulous nutrition, and an unyielding spirit. These people earn the title of real natural bodybuilders through their hard work and dedication.

By steering clear of substances that alter the body’s natural equilibrium, they embark on a journey where authenticity is the brushstroke that defines their physical artistry.

How do they Test if someone is Natty?

To make it to our best natural bodybuilders list is no joke – and this means no doping! However, there is no set protocol in the world of bodybuilding that helps distinguish natural bodybuilders from the others.

While WADA does have methods of testing, they hold no jurisdiction over bodybuilders. That said, there are a few methods in which WADA can do testing:

Urine test: The athlete will choose a urine sample collection vessel from a selection made available by the doping control personnel

Blood test: If a blood sample is collected, the athlete will choose a blood collection kit from a selection made available by the doping control personnel

Then, the athlete’s sealed sample is secured and sent to a WADA-accredited laboratory. A blood sample collected as part of the ABP program may be analyzed by a WADA-approved laboratory.

The laboratory copy of the DCF that accompanies the sample is anonymized, indicating only the sample bottle number, sport, and the athlete’s gender.

How To Know if Someone is Using Steroids?

So, your buddy or boyfriend has suddenly gotten really strong or jacked and you suspect they might be using the juice.

What are the signs to look out for? Well, there are quite a few:

Bruises and swelling: Most Steroid users will use an injectable version which means they might have bruises or swollen areas around their body, especially the shoulders and buttocks.

Extreme Muscle Mass: If someone gains an incredible amount of muscle mass in a short span of time then chances are they are using some kind of steroid or growth hormone.

Behavioural changes: Steroids can also impact the user’s mental health leaving them more irritable and aggressive – also known as ‘roid rage.’

Acne and Hair Loss: One of the most common side-effects of using steroids is acne, especially around the shoulders and back. Hair loss is also to be expected.

Vascularity and Muscle Definition: A lot of muscle striations or high vascularity which is consistently visible is a sign of body-enhancing drugs.
