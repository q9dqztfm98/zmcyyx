Salomon XT-6

Things were changed when Salomon released XT-6 back in 2013. The style is everything you can ask for in Salomon: technology-backed design and consistent comfort. As a pair of running shoes, the XT-6 has an aggressive outsole and multidirectional lugs built for heavy-duty activities on foot, and the lightweight upper locks your feet in place while keeping them cool with quick-drying materials. We especially love the brand's SensiFit™ technology which cradles your foot for a secure and precise fit that feels comfortable all day. Salomon XT-4 OG

A predecessor to the XT-6, the XT-4 OG is also a trailblazer that lets you embrace the thrill of the outdoors, and its performance is still at the top of the game after over a decade. For instance, the dual-density cushioning in the midsole lets you enjoy optimal cushioning and responsiveness, powering you through long runs or challenging hikes—and we love how it delivers an extra boost with each step in our running tests. The overall construction which Salomon calls a "Skeleton" feels lightweight yet sturdy, and we found it to be extra stable on muddy paths, rocky trails, and everything in between.

Salomon Sense Ride 5

A winner of both our Outdoor Awards and Fitness Awards, the Sense Ride is a versatile trail running shoe that will help you go fast on short distances, and go steady on long runs. We were truly amazed by how smooth a trail run could be with these on our feet. The very grippy outsole keeps you grounded regardless of terrain—bumpy, wet, uneven, and the like. The responsive midsole feels extra plush but also has shock absorption, which means you can spend less energy on your feet and more maxing out your breath. The mesh upper, lastly, also grabs our attention thanks to its lightweight build made with Sensifit which provides just the right amount of comfortable hold without pressing on your skin.

Salomon XT-WINGS 2

Having claimed its cult footwear status in recent years, Salomon also has joined the bandwagon of retro sneakers and dad shoes, like the XT-WINGS which has everything you’d expect from Salomon: durable, breathable, and waterproof.

Comfort: Cushioning plays a crucial role in keeping your feet happy during long hours of activity. Look for Salomon sneakers that provide ample support and shock absorption in the midsole, which should feel plush and responsive, to minimize fatigue and prevent discomfort.

Fit: In all of our Salomon sneaker testing, we've found the brand's sizes run true to size for a perfect fit right out of the box, so no need to go up or down a size. However, some very performance-oriented models have thick uppers which might squeeze your feet a bit. The good thing about Salomon shoes is that they pretty much all use the Quicklace system to allow for easy adjustments and a snug fit that stays in place while you're on the move.

Traction: Since many Salomon sneakers target trail running and hiking or all in all treading on mixed terrains, we feel that the grip of each pair's outsole is pretty solid.
