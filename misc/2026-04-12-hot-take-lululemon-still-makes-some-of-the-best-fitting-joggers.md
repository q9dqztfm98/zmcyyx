Frequently Asked Questions About Lululemon Men's Pants

Which Lululemon joggers are the best for men?

The best joggers come down to what you're looking to get out of your athleisure pants. If you want a pair to carry you to, from, and inside the gym, we'd recommend the City Sweat Jogger. The fabric is breathable and quick-drying to keep you comfortable throughout warm-ups and cool-downs. If you're looking for an every day pant, the ABC Jogger was designed with an added gusset to create comfortable wear as you move throughout your day.

Do Lululemon joggers run big or small?

The majority of Lululemon's pants run true to size, and many come in extended sizing and multiple-length options. The brand also lists the fit of each pant, so if you're hunting for a classic tapered look, a relaxed feel, or a more slim cut, you've got options to choose from. If reviewers noted that sizing ran small, we've made a note of it in our breakdown.

Are Lululemon joggers worth the investment?

Lululemon's joggers are no exception to the overall high quality and durability of the brand's offerings. Our editors have worn, washed, and worn the joggers again to get a sense of how they stand up to everyday wear and tear. To no surprise, the pants, which run over $100, maintain their shape, softness, and overall feel, even after multiple washes, so we'd say their worth the investment.

We're fans of the proprietary fabrics, detail in design, and versatile style options that give wearers of all different body types and needs a jogger they can rely on. The brand is an editor favorite across a wide range of categories, and these joggers are yet another reason why.
