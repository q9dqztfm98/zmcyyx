What to Look for in Workout Pants

Finding the best workout pants will mostly come down to what sort of exercise you engage in, as well as any personal likes or dislikes you may have regarding the style of pants. General factors related to how clothes should function when you’ve increased physical movement—such as comfort, material flexibility, breathability and resilience—are pretty universal.

Many brands have honed in on these requirements by developing gear that’s lightweight, wicks moisture and tackles odor. Workout pants that transcend the realm of exercise and double up as lounging or casual clothes might add anti-wrinkle properties to fabrics.

That said, those who participate in specific exercises—take runners and hikers who often spend time outside, for example—might take to more advanced gear, like wind- and water-resistant materials and UPF (Ultraviolet Protection Factor) ratings to guard them from the elements.

Weightlifters and yoga practitioners, on the other hand, should prioritize ultra-stretchy and more form-fitting fabrics to support their precise movements. Regardless of where or how you work out, secure pockets for keeping your belongings are almost always going to be a win when you’re more active.

Who Should Wear Workout Pants?

There's a variety of movements and workouts that specifically call for workout pants. However, athleisure wear is no longer reserved only for gym-goers and rather acceptable for personal style. Workout pants are suitable for anyone running errands, attending happy hour with friends, traveling on long flights, and more.

Our active guys who are runners should have a pair of workout pants in their arsenal for the coldest winter months to brave the elements. If pilates or yoga is a part of your physical routine, it would be practical to wear them as this particular exercise requires moving different body parts and landing in several poses. Workout pants are also fitting for anyone that partakes in the following activities: weight-training, HIIT, cycling, beach sports.

Are Joggers Good for the Gym?

Once again, this might come down to personal choice, but overall, joggers are considered a good fit for the gym. Firstly, they’re comfortable. It’s as simple as that. Secondly, in case of accidents, they act as a direct protective barrier between your legs (especially your vulnerable shins and knees) and potentially injurious gym apparatus or environmental factors. They also reduce the risk of exposure to pathogens like bacteria and viruses that can enter damaged skin from unclean exercise equipment.

Other practical advantages of joggers come down to their material weight. Heavier joggers can trap heat, which warms up your muscles faster. Thicker joggers are also useful if you’d intentionally like to sweat more during a workout. With that in mind, more lightweight joggers still protect your lower half, but can be found in more breathable fabrics.
