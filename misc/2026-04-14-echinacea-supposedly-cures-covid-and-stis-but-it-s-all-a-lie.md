Does echinacea treat STIs?

And, yeah, echinacea supplements also probably won’t do anything for STIs. The myth makes the rounds in homeopathic circles, and the little research that exists is on varying dosages. Beware any over-the-counter supplement (or medical “expert”) stating that echinacea treats herpes, HIV/AIDS, or UTIs. There’s no consistent research yet.

Does echinacea protect from Covid?

Scientists haven’t proved that echinacea prevents or treats Covid-19. Both the cold and Covid are viral respiratory illnesses, but Covid-19 is caused by SARS-CoV-2, and colds are often caused by rhinoviruses. There is currently no research showing that echinacea supplements protect you from Covid. Masks and vaccinations do that.
