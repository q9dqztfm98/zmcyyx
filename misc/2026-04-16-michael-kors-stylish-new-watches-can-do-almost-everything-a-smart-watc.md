# Michael Kors’ Stylish New Watches Can Do Almost Everything a Smart Watch Can

There are two types of watches: You have the smart watch—digital, multipurpose, capable of hailing an Uber, and you have the classic wristwatch—analog, stylish, blissfully ignorant.

But what if you didn’t have to choose between smart and traditional?

Michael Kors Access is the new collection of stylishly robust smart watches from designer Michael Kors.

Unveiled this week, the Access collection offers two versions—the women’s Bradshaw and the men’s Dylan (pictured above)—with a selection of interchangeable silicon straps to match (and likely elevate) your style.

(You know what will look great with these? The Men’s Health logo T-shirt. It’s guaranteed to be as soft as your favorite washed-a-million-times tee—right out of the package.)

It’s durable but refined in its design, and transitions seamlessly from a work to weekend wardrobe. But not only is this watch deserving of an Instagram post, you can also use it TO check out your feed.

The Michael Kors Access piece can connect to your Apple or Android smartphone, as well as many of the apps on it. You can receive notifications on the display screen from calls, texts, or even your Uber driver.

There’s also a built-in, basic fitness tracker to show you your steps taken, distance traveled, and calories burned—all while looking like a traditional timepiece.

Our favorite feature, however, is the ability to set the watch face to change automatically as day transitions to night, so you can go from a sporty to dressier look without touching a button. The wireless magnetic charging is a nice touch, too.

The Michael Kors Access watch is available starting today, online and at select retailers worldwide ($350, michaelkors.com).
