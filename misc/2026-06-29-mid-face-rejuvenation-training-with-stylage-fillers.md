# Mid Face Rejuvenation Training with Stylage Fillers

Preview

About this lesson

Learn effective techniques for restoring mid-face projection, lift, and contour in patients experiencing age-related volume loss.

In this Level B training, Dr. Juan Sopena demonstrates a structured approach to mid-face rejuvenation using Stylage XL for lateral cheekbone support and Stylage M for the malar region. You’ll learn how to assess volume loss, facial sagging, and zygomatic contour before selecting the right product based on G prime and treatment goals. Throughout the procedure, Dr. Sopena focuses on symmetry, projection improvement, and harmonizing the entire mid-face.
