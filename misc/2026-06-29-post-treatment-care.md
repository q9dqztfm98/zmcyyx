Tattoo Removal Post-Treatment Instructions

1. Immediately after the treatment, your skin will probably be red, swollen, and slightly raised. The sensation will likely be similar to that of a sunburn.2. Keep the area clean, use mild, unscented soap to wash daily. Avoid excessive sweating in the area treated.3. Protect the treated area from friction of clothing & avoid picking or scratching.4. A thin layer of antibiotic ointment will be provided by the practitioner if needed.5. Stay hydrated. Over the next 10 weeks, the tattoo ink will slowly start to break down, and you may not notice any changes. This is normal. Over time and with each treatment, the edges of the tattoo will begin to fade.6. Sunlight can irritate the skin and slow recovery/results. Stay out of direct sunlight and use a high-SPF (>30) sunscreen when you must go outside.7. Treatments should be 8- 10 weeks apart. The number of treatments is dependent on several factors which may include the following:

Ink used

Technique used

Colors used

How old the tattoo is

Immune system

Each visit your provider will talk with you on how the results are going and what to expect with each treatment.

Medical Disclaimer: This post‑care information is provided for general guidance and does not replace professional medical evaluation or treatment. If you experience symptoms that may indicate a serious or potentially life‑threatening condition, seek immediate medical care.
