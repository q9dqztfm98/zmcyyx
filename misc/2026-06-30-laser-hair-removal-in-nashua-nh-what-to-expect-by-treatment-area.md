How Laser Hair Removal Works

Laser hair removal directs light energy at the pigment in each hair follicle. The follicle absorbs the energy, which reduces its ability to produce new hair. The treatment is most effective on follicles in their active growth phase at the time of treatment.

Because follicles cycle through phases at different times, a single session reaches only a portion of them. A series spaced over weeks treats more follicles for a more complete result. See the laser hair removal page for an overview.

This growth cycle is why patience pays off. Rushing sessions too close together does not treat more follicles, so following the spacing your provider recommends is what makes the series effective.

How Many Sessions You May Need

Most patients need a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart to address hair across its growth cycle. The exact number depends on the area, hair type, and individual response, so a provider outlines a realistic plan at your consultation.

Missing appointments or spacing them too far apart can slow progress, because you may miss follicles during their active phase. Keeping to your provider’s schedule helps you get the most from each visit.
