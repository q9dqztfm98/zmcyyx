What Is PRF EZ Gel?

PRF EZ Gel is an advanced formulation of platelet-rich fibrin, processed through a controlled heating and cooling cycle to create a gel matrix. This autologous solution is rich in fibrin, growth factors, and cytokines-elements critical for tissue healing and collagen stimulation.

Unlike traditional PRF or PRP, the gel form allows for better control, precision placement, and slower resorption. As it contains no synthetic components, PRF EZ Gel significantly reduces the risk of allergic reactions or long-term filler complications. This makes it an attractive option for under-eye rejuvenation without HA fillers.

PRF EZ Gel Side Effects

PRF EZ Gel is generally well-tolerated, especially due to its autologous nature. Most patients experience only mild and temporary symptoms. The most commonly reported side effects include:

Mild redness, swelling, or tenderness around the injection site.

Slight bruising, which usually resolves without intervention.

Temporary skin sensitivity or a feeling of tightness around the treated area.

Occasional itching or dryness during the healing phase.

Rarely, patients may experience a mild headache or temporary pressure sensation due to the delicate anatomy of the under-eye region.

FAQs

How painful is PRF EZ Gel?

Discomfort is minimal. Most patients describe the treatment as tolerable, especially when using a blunt cannula and topical numbing. The sensation is typically brief and resolves quickly post-procedure.

Is PRF or Sculptra better?

It depends on the goal. PRF is ideal for patients seeking natural, autologous rejuvenation, while Sculptra offers longer-lasting collagen stimulation in larger areas. Treatment selection should consider the patient’s tissue quality, goals, and injection area.

Is EZ Gel better than fillers?

For the under-eye area, PRF EZ Gel may offer a more natural and safer alternative to HA fillers, especially in patients prone to puffiness or complications. It also improves skin quality over time, rather than simply replacing lost volume.

How to sleep after EZ Gel?

Patients should sleep on their back with the head slightly elevated for the first 1-2 nights to reduce swelling and promote even distribution. This helps the gel integrate smoothly and minimizes pressure on the treated area.

Can EZ Gel migrate?

Migration is extremely rare due to the gel’s cohesive nature and precise placement technique. When administered correctly, the gel remains localized and integrates effectively with surrounding tissue.
