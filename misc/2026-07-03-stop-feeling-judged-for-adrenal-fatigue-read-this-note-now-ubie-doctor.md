Adrenal fatigue is a term used to describe a collection of symptoms—such as tiredness, brain fog, low mood, and difficulty handling stress—that some believe result from prolonged stress overworking the adrenal glands. While adrenal fatigue is not recognized as a medical diagnosis, the symptoms people experience are very real and can significantly impact daily life. How you interpret and respond to these symptoms can shape your emotions, stress levels, and overall well-being.
