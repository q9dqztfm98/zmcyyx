How Resveratrol Works: Antioxidant Protection

At its core, resveratrol serves as a potent antioxidant, meaning it helps neutralize free radicals—unstable molecules that can damage cells and contribute to aging, inflammation, and chronic disease. Several mechanisms explain why your doctor values resveratrol for total body defense:

Neutralizing Free Radicals

Resveratrol donates electrons to free radicals, stabilizing them and preventing them from harming DNA, proteins, and cell membranes.

Enhancing Endogenous Antioxidant Enzymes

It upregulates enzymes such as superoxide dismutase (SOD) and catalase, boosting your body's own defense system.

Reducing Inflammation

By modulating inflammatory pathways (e.g., NF-κB), resveratrol can lower chronic, low-grade inflammation linked to conditions like arthritis, metabolic syndrome, and cardiovascular disease.

Dosage and Forms

Typical supplement range: 100–500 mg per day, often divided into two doses.

Food sources:• A glass of red wine (150 mL) provides roughly 1–2 mg of resveratrol—far below supplement levels.• A handful of peanuts or berries adds small amounts but shouldn't replace supplements if higher doses are desired.

Standardized extracts: Look for products that list pure trans-resveratrol content to ensure potency.

Always follow product instructions and your doctor's guidance. Quality varies among brands; choose supplements tested by independent labs for purity.

Safety and Potential Interactions

Resveratrol is generally well tolerated, but consider these points:

Side effects:• Digestive upset (e.g., gas, nausea) at high doses.• Headache or dizziness in sensitive individuals.

Blood thinners: Resveratrol may enhance the effects of anticoagulant medications (e.g., warfarin).

Hormone-sensitive conditions: Although resveratrol has weak estrogen-like activity, its clinical impact is minimal at standard doses. If you have hormone-sensitive cancer, discuss it with your doctor.

Medication interactions: Always review your full medication list with your healthcare provider before starting supplements.
