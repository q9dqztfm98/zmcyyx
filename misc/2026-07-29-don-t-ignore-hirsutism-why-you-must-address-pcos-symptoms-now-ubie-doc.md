Explanation

Hirsutism is a condition where women grow hair in areas where men typically do, such as the face, chest, and back. This happens because of high levels of male hormones called androgens. One common cause of hirsutism is Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS).

What is PCOS?

PCOS is a condition that affects a woman's hormone levels.

[Now Ubie Doctor's Note](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Women with PCOS often have higher levels of androgens, which can lead to hirsutism.

How are Hirsutism and PCOS Linked?

Many women with PCOS experience hirsutism due to the increased androgen levels.

PCOS can also cause irregular periods and problems with fertility.
