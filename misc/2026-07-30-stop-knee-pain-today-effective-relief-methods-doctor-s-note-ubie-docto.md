Explanation

There are several ways to relieve knee pain without surgery. One important method is exercise. Low-impact activities such as swimming, walking, or cycling can strengthen the muscles around the knee and reduce stress on the joint. Regular stretching and strengthening exercises, often taught in physical therapy, help improve flexibility and support the knee better. Weight management is also very helpful because losing extra weight can lessen the pressure on the knee joint.

Some people find relief with physical treatments like acupuncture, which might help reduce pain and improve movement. If you're experiencing persistent knee discomfort and want to understand what might be causing it, you can take a free symptom checker assessment to get personalized insights. Over-the-counter pain relievers or anti-inflammatory medications can be used to manage pain when needed.

Using proper techniques during exercise and daily activities, like wearing supportive shoes or using a knee brace, can also protect the knee and reduce discomfort. These conservative, nonsurgical treatments work together to lower pain levels and build a stronger, more stable knee, making everyday tasks easier and often reducing the need for more invasive treatments.
