When someone with multiple myeloma stands up after sitting, they might feel lightheaded due to a drop in blood pressure known as orthostatic hypotension. This condition happens when the body struggles to adjust blood flow quickly enough to the brain upon standing.

Why does this happen?

Blood Pressure Drop: Orthostatic hypotension occurs when blood pressure falls suddenly when standing up.

[Up Can Wait Ubie](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/7751602840.html)

Slow Adjustment: The body may not adjust blood flow to the brain quickly enough, causing dizziness.

How is this related to multiple myeloma?

Multiple myeloma can affect the blood and nervous system, potentially contributing to issues like orthostatic hypotension.

It's important for healthcare providers to recognize and manage this condition to prevent falls and other complications.

Understanding these symptoms and their causes can help individuals with multiple myeloma manage their condition more effectively.
