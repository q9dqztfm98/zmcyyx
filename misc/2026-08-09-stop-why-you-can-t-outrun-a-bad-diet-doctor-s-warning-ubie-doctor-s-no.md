Being physically active is important, but it is not enough to balance out unhealthy eating habits. Exercise helps keep our bodies fit and healthy, but what we eat plays a big role in our overall health.

Why Diet Matters

Energy Balance: Eating too much unhealthy food can lead to gaining weight, even if you exercise a lot.

Nutrients: Healthy foods give your body the nutrients it needs to work well. Junk food does not have these nutrients.
