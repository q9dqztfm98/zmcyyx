What Is Hemoglobin? Why Your Blood Is Vital & Medically Approved Next Steps

Hemoglobin is the iron-rich protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen to tissues and returns carbon dioxide to the lungs. What Is POTS? Why Your Heart Is Spiking & Medical Next Steps

Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS) is an autonomic nervous system disorder where standing up causes your heart rate to jump 30 bpm or more without a significant blood pressure drop. Blood pools in the lower body, forcing the heart to race, which triggers dizziness, palpitations, fatigue, and brain fog. What to do next: Track your lying vs. standing heart rate, and see a clinician for orthostatic vitals, tilt table testing, and labs to rule out other causes. Start hydration, appropriate salt intake, compression garments, and gradual recumbent exercise. Seek urgent care for crushing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or sudden fainting.
