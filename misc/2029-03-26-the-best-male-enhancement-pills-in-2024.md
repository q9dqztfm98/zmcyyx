Safety

The safety profiles of these various male enhancement products are a mixed bag. Since many of the studies looking into common male enhancement ingredients often lack large enough sample sizes to say anything positive with confidence, it’s also difficult to say how safe these products may or may not be. In many cases, male enhancement pills use doses of individual ingredients that are lower than those seen in studies. That might be a good thing for their safety, but without research into how these ingredients may interact when taken together — even at lower doses, and particularly over a long period of time — it would be irresponsible to declare them safe.

We are aware of certain side effects that crop up in studies examining these ingredients’ potential to treat ED, but some boast studies specific to their safety. What are male enhancement pills?

Male enhancement pills are nutritional supplements designed to improve various aspects of male sexual health. Many focus on erectile performance, seeking to improve blood flow to the penis and address certain nutritional deficiencies that might contribute to mild erectile dysfunction. They typically include botanical ingredients like ginseng and horny goat weed, as well as amino acids like L-arginine and L-carnitine. Some also offer vitamins and minerals.

Technically, the category includes the ridiculous and often dangerous types of pills you see sold in gas stations and truck stops around America. These are not the types of male enhancement pills we’ll focus on here. It doesn’t guarantee their efficacy, but the supplements in our guide come from more reputable companies than those hawked next to novelty lighters and beef jerky.

Other types of supplements exist under the male enhancement umbrella, including volume pills and testosterone boosters. These products often share several ingredients with traditional male enhancement pills, but their formulas tend to focus on increasing semen output and raising testosterone levels, respectively.

Ultimately, even the best male enhancement pill can only go so far in addressing things like erectile dysfunction. If you have only mild symptoms (e.g., the ability to get mostly erect but not enough for penetration, or your erectile performance is mostly reliable but a tad inconsistent), male enhancement pills may be able to help. But most men — especially those with moderate-to-severe ED — would do better to seek out prescription-strength assistance.

There is a common misconception that male enhancement pills are intended to make your penis larger. This is not the case. While it is possible for increased blood flow to the penis to result in an erection that’s marginally larger than you’re used to, the physical structures of your penis will not grow in any way.

Here’s a look at some key ingredients you’ll find in many male enhancement pills:

L-Arginine HCL

This amino acid is a popular ingredient in male enhancement pills to help men get and maintain firm erections. It enables the body to produce nitric oxide, which increases blood flow to the penis.6 Studies indicate it may improve sexual function in men, but often with higher doses than seen in supplements, often between 2g and 5g daily.

Epimedium leaf

This ingredient is also called barrenwort or horny goat weed. It can act as a modest phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE-5) inhibitor — the same essential function as Viagra and Cialis.7 However, most studies look into icariin, a component extracted from epimedium, at doses around 1,000mg.8 Epimedium in male enhancement pills contains about 10% icariin, so a 1,000mg dose of epimedium leaf extract is only worth one-tenth of the amount that succeeds in studies.

Ginseng

Ginseng may work on the body in several ways, modulating neuronal and hormonal systems and even increasing spermatogenesis, at least in rodent studies.39 40 41 Studies show improvements in erectile strength and maintenance and overall libido at doses around 1,000mg daily.9

Zinc

Many studies link zinc with testosterone levels.10 Since men lose testosterone production as they age, supplementing with zinc and other testosterone-boosting ingredients can improve various sexual functions.

Maca

Maca is a popular supplement ingredient that can only claim inconclusive results for men’s sexual function when looking at multiple studies.11 Beyond potentially boosting libido and fertility, it can positively affect mood and blood pressure.12

Muira puama

Evidence supporting muira puama’s role in sexual health is severely limited, and most of the studies looking into its potential in humans involve multi-ingredient supplements.13 At this time, we can’t say that this ingredient should sway your decision one way or another.

Tribulus terrestris

You’ll find this ingredient in many male enhancement pills and testosterone supplements. Research into it can be contradictory,14 with some studies suggesting considerable benefits to blood sugar and sexual health and others coming up inconclusive.15

Ashwagandha

This is one of the most reliable testosterone-boosting ingredients you’ll find in men’s supplements.2 It isn’t in many male enhancement pills, but you should take note when it is. Claims around MSM’s ability to grow your penis or cure erectile dysfunction are significantly overblown. There is little clinical evidence to support them, though there is some oblique evidence linking sulfur (which you can derive from MSM) to the relaxation of the corpus cavernosum.18

Some companies include a black pepper extract, sometimes labeled as piperine or under the brand name Bioperine. Are male enhancement pills safe?

We’ve done our best to bring you a collection that meets a high bar for safety with minimal adverse effects. But not all male enhancement pills are safe, and there are ingredients in even some of the safest options that could pose a danger to you.

Prescription ED medications (PDE-5 inhibitors like Viagra, specifically) can lower blood pressure.20 This is why any prescriber needs a recent blood pressure reading and knowledge of your cardiovascular health history before prescribing them. Supplemental male enhancement pills often employ ingredients that aim to act in similar ways. That makes them potentially dangerous for men with low blood pressure or men taking medication that affects their blood pressure.

Other ingredients in supplements may have unexpected consequences for certain men. For example, ashwagandha has been linked to increased thyroid hormone production, which could lead to hyperthyroidism in some.21

Some ingredients bring the possibility of mild adverse reactions, but they’re worth knowing.

MSM: nausea, diarrhea, bloating, stomach pain

Ginseng: nervousness, insomnia

L-Arginine: bloating, stomach pain, gout

Epimedium leaf: irregular heartbeat, low blood pressure

Take a close look at the ingredients in the pills you have your eye on, and talk to your doctor before starting it to make sure there are no unexpected risks to your health or potential contraindications with any of your current medications.
