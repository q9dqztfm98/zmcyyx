Where to get a rapid PCR test for traveling from Sacramento

There are several places you can go to receive a rapid PCR test in Sacramento. For a list of free PCR testing sites and the days and times they are available, the Sacramento County website offers a free testing site finder.

If you would like to visit a drive-through testing site, you could visit Covid Clinic’s Sacramento Testing Site at 1801 3rd Street, Sacramento, CA, 95811. They are available Monday through Saturday from 7 am to 7 pm and also on Sunday from 8 am through 4 pm.

You can also get a rapid PCR test at Sacramento International Airport. It’s important to note that travelers need to make an appointment 48 hours in advance. If you have a valid travel itinerary, you will receive priority booking. The testing location is in the airport parking lot between AMPM and the East Economy Lot (formerly the taxi waiting area).
