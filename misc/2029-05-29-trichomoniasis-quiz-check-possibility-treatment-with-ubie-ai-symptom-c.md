What is Trichomoniasis?

[Quiz Check Possibility Treatment](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Infection of the vagina by a parasite called Trichomonas. The disease is sexually transmitted but often does not cause any symptoms in men.

Typical Symptoms of Trichomoniasis

Increased vaginal discharge that is smelly

Foamy vaginal discharge

Pain or burning during urination

Increase in vaginal discharge

Pus comes out from urethra - the opening when urine exits from

Having difficulty urinating or producing less urine

Abnormal color, amount or odor of vaginal discharge

Burning or painful urination with reduced urine volume

Diagnostic Questions for Trichomoniasis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Has the vaginal discharge odor worsened recently?

Is your vaginal discharge foamy?

Are you experiencing pain or burning when you urinate?

Have you had more vaginal discharge lately?

Is there pus coming out of your urethra?

Treatment of Trichomoniasis

Anti-parasite medication may be given to treat the infection. This medication is safe for use during pregnancy.

Q.

Internal Fire? Why Trichomoniasis Stings & Your Medical Next Steps

A.

Burning or stinging that feels like an internal fire plus unusual discharge can be caused by trichomoniasis, a very common but curable STI; the parasite inflames sensitive genital tissue, which makes urination and sex painful. There are several factors to consider, including that it often has no symptoms, can be diagnosed quickly with accurate tests, and is cured with antibiotics that you and your partners need while pausing sex. See below for the exact next steps that matter, including when to seek urgent care, how to prevent reinfection, key medication cautions like avoiding alcohol with metronidazole or tinidazole, and prevention and screening tips that could change your plan.
