# This 5-Minute Boxing-Inspired Dumbbell Workout Packs a Punch

EVEN IF YOU’RE only working out for five minutes, you’ll get bored doing the same old thing over and over again. Sure, you can push yourself hard doing bodyweight exercises like pushups and air squats (as we’ve shown you before), but you’ll have a better time—and a better sweat—if you switch things up and train for some more specific goals.

That’s the concept behind this hard-hitting, boxing-inspired dumbbell session from trainer David Pegram, NASM-CPT, creator of the Power Up program. The workout mixes strength training exercises like plank rows and goblet squats with sport-specific movements like dumbbell uppercuts and speed punches to hone your striking power. Given the fast, intense setup, you’ll push yourself to improve your conditioning to help ready yourself for the ring, too.

The session is set up to challenge you to get stronger as it goes on. All of the rounds are for time; the is a minute and split into two 30-second sections, the second extends to 90 seconds split into 45 second sections, and the third is two minutes, split into two 45 second sections and a 30 second finisher.

The key here: producing power through your hips and core. This will help if you have any Gold Gloves aspirations—but it can be just as helpful if you just want to get moving and break free of your standard workouts. Just don’t pick up weights that are too heavy. The near non-stop activity and the volume of reps will provide more than enough of a challenge.
