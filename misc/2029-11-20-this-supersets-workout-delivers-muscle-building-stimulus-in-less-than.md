# This Supersets Workout Delivers Muscle-Building Stimulus in Less Than 30 Minutes

IF YOU WANT to build visible muscle fast, your training plan needs supersets. Unlike straight sets that allot you anywhere from 30 seconds to two minutes rest before you get moving again, supersets eliminate the downtime between exercises. The result? More hypertrophy and faster gains.

But you can’t throw just any series of exercises in a superset. You need moves that complement each other and won’t cause a faster rate of fatigue accumulation. This Mega Muscle Superset workout from Don Saladino’s Superhero Shred program hits the sweet spot, no planning required.
