# NFL Star Julian Edelman Is Hosting a Live At-Home Workout Tonight

New England Patriots wide receiver Julian Edelman is teaming up with Planet Fitness today to bring you a live at-home workout to help keep you fit during the coronavirus quarantine. Edelman, a Super Bowl MVP and three-time champion, is a prime candidate to help you to stay fit.

The Edelman workout will be a 20-minute, equipment-free muscle burner. The receiver will go live on Planet Fitness' Facebook page at 7 p.m. EST. Planet Fitness has also been streaming live (and free) 'work-ins' every day at 7 p.m. EST. With fan-favorite Edelman on board to host, this one is bound to be extra special and entertaining.

People are pumped about the opportunity to sweat with a football star.
