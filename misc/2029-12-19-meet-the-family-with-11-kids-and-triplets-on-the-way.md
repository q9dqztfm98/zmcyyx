Imagine you and your partner had eleven young children. Now imagine it was up to you to take care of them all—because your partner is in the hospital getting ready to give birth to three more.

That's reality for Deon Derrico, the father of (now) 14 children and star of TLC's new docuseries, Doubling Down With the Derricos. The show, which premiered Aug. 11, follows Deon and his wife, Karen, as they work to parent their massive brood, consisting of quintuplets, triplets, and two sets of twins—all of them conceived naturally.
