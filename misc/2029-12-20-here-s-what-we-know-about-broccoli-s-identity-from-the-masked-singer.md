What clues do we know about Broccoli?

In Broccoli’s intro package on a teaser episode of The Masked Singer, fans got a good helping of clues which hint at this vegetable’s true identity.

“I’m a Brocco-lean, mean, heart-pumping machine,” Broccoli says as a main clue. “After all, it’s how I got my six-pack.”

In the package, Broccoli also says they are moving from “a side dish to a main course meal,” suggesting they may often be on the sidelines in their career.

Notably, Broccoli is also wearing a letterman jacket. That outfit choice coupled with the “six-pack” claims could indicate this performer is an athlete.

Who is Broccoli on The Masked Singer?

Given Broccoli’s deep voice, which is evident even with identity-protecting distortion, some fans believe the contestant could be actor Seth Rogen. Others have floated the idea of George Foreman, due to his boxing past and the iconic George Foreman Grill catchphrase claiming the device is a “lean, mean, fat-reducing grilling machine.”

Other fans believe singer Khalid is behind the mask due to Broccoli’s costume resembling a sort of caricature of the performer's hair and usual clothing—plus that deep voice. Finally, Vin Diesel, Channing Tatum and Hulk Hogan were also brought up as possible celebrities behind the mask just for good measure.
