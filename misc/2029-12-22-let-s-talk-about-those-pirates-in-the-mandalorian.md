# Let’s Talk About Those Pirates in ‘The Mandalorian’

Season 2, episode 7 of The Mandalorian, entitled "The Believer," did not give us any new Baby Yoda content, instead taking Din Djarin and his motley crew of badasses on one final side-quest before they attempt their rescue of Grogu in the upcoming finale. And in order to track down the location of bad guy Moff Gideon, they had to team up with the mercenary and former Imperial soldier, Mayfeld, played by guest star Bill Burr.

The undercover mission, which saw Mando and Mayfeld posing as stormtroopers, took viewers behind enemy lines and depicted an uncomfortable reality of wartime: those on the other side are people, just like us. Officers want to sit around and drink and talk about old times. And when you're under attack from marauding forces on the road while transporting Imperial cargo, the appearance of TIE fighters—enemy vessels under any other circumstances—would definitely be received more warmly, as signified by the triumphant music which plays over the scene where the alien pirates are blasted into oblivion.

But wait. Who were those pirates, exactly?

As a new video on the EckhartsLadder YouTube channel points out, these nameless antagonists weren't technically pirates or raiders at all, because they weren't trying to steal anything. They ambushed the truck because they were trying to destroy its highly volatile contents, not profit from it.

[Those Pirates](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/1246586997.html)

And given what we later learned about how those materials were intended to be used (think weapons of mass destruction), the goal of the "pirates" actually seems like a pretty noble one. Which would mean that our protagonist just killed a load of freedom fighters who were trying to limit the Empire's ability to destroy more worlds.
