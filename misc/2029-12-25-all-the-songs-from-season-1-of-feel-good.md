# All the Songs From Season 1 of 'Feel Good'

Feel Good debuted on Netflix in mid March.

The show, which focuses on exploring new love and relationships and dealing with addiction, stars Canadian comedian Mae Martin.

The show also features an eclectic soundtrack, which we've rounded up right here on this page.

If you've already binged and rewatched through all your longtime favorite comfort shows on Netflix, it's worth considering Feel Good, a tightly-plotted and very funny six-part series that released earlier this month. Starring Canadian comedian Mae Martin, the show looks at a stand-up comedian who begins a new relationship with a woman who's never been anything but straight before. Meanwhile, she's also navigating her own addiction troubles in life, and balancing input from her nosy parents (with her mother being played by Friends star Lisa Kudrow!). The show feels like a new take on several other 'semi-autobiographical' series like Fleabag, and, like Fleabag, focuses intently on its troubled and often-hysterical main character. Unlike Fleabag, though, Feel Good also includes a rather impressively-selected soundtrack of licensed songs.

At only six half-hour episodes, Feel Good is a quick watch. But with storylines and themes focusing on universal concepts like relationships, new and at-times experimental love, and wrestling with addiction, it's a show that could find a way to appeal to just about everyone. The show's soundtrack—with alternative rock stalwarts like The National and Fleet Foxes included—feels like a perfect fit to match the show's laid back mood.
