# A YouTuber Made 'John Wick' Even Cooler by Adding Lightsabers

Considering its origins as a pretty straightforward revenge story, the John Wick action movie franchise could easily have continued churning out boilerplate sequels. Instead, it has swiftly created a fictional world steeped in arcane lore which becomes more complex with each new chapter. The films take place within an unseen demimonde, populated by assassins who adhere to all kinds of ancient traditions, and abide by a certain code. In that way, the Keanu Reeves-fronted series isn't that far removed from the Star Wars movies, which boast a similarly mysterious order of warriors in the form of Jedi Knights.

So, in a move which absolutely nobody asked for but yielded some pretty amazing results, special effects YouTube channel ImmersionVFX put a lightsaber in Keanu Reeves' hand. Using footage from the most recent installment, John Wick Chapter 3 - Parabellum, editors digitally inserted lightsabers into a series of sword fights from the movie, turning what was already a hi-octane thrill ride into a neon-infused glimpse at just how well-suited the preternaturally chill Keanu would be to playing a Jedi if the inclination ever arose.

First up is a chase scene in which John is pursued by what appear to be a group of Sith on motorcycles. Sith! On! Motorbikes! Come on, you have to admit that's cooler than the fat amount of nothing the Knights of Ren ended up doing in the sequel trilogy. That sequence is followed by a penthouse confrontation with Zero (Mark Dacascos), which has all the villainous taunting and twirling swordsmanship you might expect to see from an epic climax featuring Darth Vader or Palpatine.
