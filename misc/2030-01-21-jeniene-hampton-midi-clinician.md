Jeniene Hampton, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

From the lab to the exam room: “I began my medical journey as a lab-based medical technologist before transitioning to cardiothoracic nursing, where I cared for patients in the hospital for seven years. Now, I am a Family Nurse Practitioner and have advanced training in obesity medicine. My varied experiences have given me a broad perspective on patient care and the tools to help women through all stages of their health journeys.”

Walking the walk with you: “My care approach was deeply influenced by experiencing infertility firsthand and now navigating midlife. I know what it feels like to be in my patients’ shoes, and I really see my patients as peers—because I’m a woman in midlife, experiencing many of the same health challenges they are.”

Evidence-based, synergistic solutions: “I’m passionate about combining alternative botanical medicine with Western medicine to create integrative Care Plans. My approach with every patient is always collaborative, ensuring we pursue treatment that aligns with her goals and needs.”
