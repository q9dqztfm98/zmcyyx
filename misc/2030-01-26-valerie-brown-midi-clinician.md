Valerie Brown, DNP, APRN, WHNP-BC

Driven by lifelong learning: “I became a Registered Nurse in 2009 and later earned my Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner certification in 2017. Fascinated by the female body, I began in public health, caring for women in their reproductive years, then transitioned to working with military members and retirees. That’s where I really dove into midlife care, seeing firsthand how much support and guidance women need during this stage.”

You deserve to be heard: “What drew me to menopause care was how many women felt dismissed. Midlife women would come to me, exhausted from not being heard, and so appreciative when I simply took the time to listen. Finding Midi, a company that shares that same mission, felt like the perfect fit.”

Care beyond just symptoms: “When you see me, you can expect a listening ear, a conversation about what matters most to you, and a plan to help you feel your best. What I love about Midi is the wealth of clinical resources that keep us at the forefront of evidence-based care. It means I can keep learning, growing, and offering my patients the most up-to-date, comprehensive support possible.”
