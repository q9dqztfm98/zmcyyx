# Ed Sheeran's Tattoos Are 'Sh*t', Says His Tattoo Artist

Tattoo artist Kevin Paul has inked a whole lot of famous people, from Rihanna to Cara Delevingne to Harry Styles. But he's probably spent the most time working with musician Ed Sheeran, who he has tattooed more than 40 times over the years. Although, it turns out, even he isn't a huge fan of Sheeran's ink.

[His Tattoo](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1191124475.html)

"I take the piss out of Ed all the time, when I’m with him, that they are sh*t," he recently told the UK's Daily Mirror. "They are!" In the interview, Paul revealed that not all of Sheeran's tattoos are his doing: some of his smaller tattoos are reportedly the handiwork of Sheeran's "manager's mate," but Paul gets blamed for those too.

One of the most common annoyances that tattoo artists experience is doing basic or unoriginal designs for their clients when what they really want is to be presented with a challenge. And while Paul concedes that Sheeran's childish tats carry a "personal" meaning to the singer, he also admits that it gets frustrating to be associated with that work.

"I was a big massive artist doing 3D-work and winning multi awards," Paul continued. "And then all of a sudden you end up doing Pingu the Penguin and f–king gingerbread men on pop stars... Everybody just thought I was sh*t, but I’m not." He concludes that being Sheeran's artist has been a double-edged sword, as it has both raised his profile but also created a skewed perception of his talents and style.

Working with Sheeran has been the "best thing to happen to my career and the worst," he said, adding: "But I don’t regret working with them as it’s changed my life and opened amazing doors for me to do great things."
