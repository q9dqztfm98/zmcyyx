# The Jo Bros Had Such an Intense Group Therapy Session They Had to Drink to Get Through It

The Jonas Brothers are currently gearing up for the release of their new album, Happiness Begins, and its accompanying documentary. They've also been hitting the promo road very hard: they recently appeared on The Late Late Show with James Corden, and are due to perform at the 2019 Billboard Music Awards on May 1. The trio also sat down for an interview with Billboard, and they revealed that in order to get through a group therapy session, they all needed some liquid courage.
