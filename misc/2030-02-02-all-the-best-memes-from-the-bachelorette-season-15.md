# All the Best Memes From 'The Bachelorette' Season 15

Warning: Spoilers ahead for The Bachelorette season 15. If you don't want to know what happens, stop reading now!

The Bachelorette is an objectively absurd show. One woman dates 30 dudes at once with the goal of finding a husband, narrowing them down each week through a series of group dates, one-on-ones, and rose ceremonies. And it's precisely the absurdity of the whole premise that makes The Bachelorette one of the meme-friendly shows on TV. Half the fun of being a Bachelorette fan is watching the two-hour show on Monday nights; the other half is reading the jokes on social media.

"Alabama Hannah" Brown is the star of the season 15, picking her way through a guy with a girlfriend already, a sperm donor who's fathered 114 children, two pilots, and a man who calls himself the "Box King." (We'll apparently be seeing the latter on Bachelor in Paradise.) Needless to say, there's plenty of fodder for A+ Bachelorette memes.
