According to Inside Survivor:

"The Survivor veterans are there to offer advice and guidance to the crop of new castaways competing on the season currently filming in Fiji. Unlike John Cochran in Game Changers, who shared advice with Debbie Wanner as part of an Exile reward, this is expected to be a season-long twist, not just a one-off appearance.

It’s currently unclear exactly how this concept will work or to what extent Rob and Sandra’s role is in the season. However, we don’t believe either returning player will be living at camp with the new castaways, at least not on a permanent basis. That would suggest the past champions are being kept separate from the cast and will only appear when called upon to give advice (perhaps as part of a reward or some sort of Exile twist)."

Entertainment Weekly later clarified the duo's role, with host Jeff Probst adding that, "They aren’t playing, they can’t cast a vote, they can’t be voted out, and they can’t win the million dollars. The basic idea is they run a Survivor boot camp."

When players are sent to the Island of the Idols they will find two living, breathing Survivor idols in Sandra and Boston Rob waiting for them. Sandra and Rob will not actually be playing the game, but instead will serve as advisors to the contestants that visit them; answering questions and offering assistance on any topic, be it strategy, shelter building, or social tips.

Boston Rob appeared on Survivor: Marquesas, Survivor: All-Stars, and Survivor: Heroes vs. Villain, and Survivor: Redemption Island, which he won. Sandra appeared on Survivor: Pearl Islands, Survivor: Heroes vs. Villains, and Survivor: Game Changers, and she's the only person to win Survivor twice.

The two were friendly on Heroes vs Villains, and they also have a bond as two former winners (could they also be on Survivor's rumored all-winners season?), so it'll be fun to see them interact with each other and with the castaways.

It also must be noted that a season of Survivor South Africa featured a similar concept in 2014. On that season, called Champions, two South African sporting legends were featured as team captions, and they drafted the two initial tribes. But while they competed in challenges pre-merge and attended Tribal Council, they couldn't vote or be voted out—but they did vote at the Final Tribal Council to help to decide the winner.

Survivor season 39 will be set in a very familiar location.
