# Laura Tubbs, Midi Clinician

[Laura Tubbs](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/9737141307.html)

[Tubbs Midi](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/blog/8071101565.html)

[Laura Tubbs](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/)

Laura Tubbs, MSN, APRN, AGPCNP-C

Drawn to women’s health: "I started working in healthcare in 2015 and focused on adult gerontology in 2019. I’ve worked in aesthetics dermatology, physical and regenerative medicine, pain management, and women’s health, including gynecology and urogynecology. Throughout all of these experiences, women’s health has always felt like home."

Creating change, little by little: "One of the most rewarding parts of my work is hearing women describe the moment things finally start to change. I’ll never forget a patient who told me she felt like herself again after months of struggling. The relief on her face made it clear just how life-altering the right support can be. Moments like that remind me why I do this work: helping women reclaim pieces of their lives, even in small steps, is profoundly meaningful."

Showing up for patients: "My goal is to make every patient feel seen and heard. I want them to leave our visit feeling informed, supported, and never rushed or dismissed. I advocate for my patients while keeping safety at the center of every decision. I start by gathering a full picture of someone’s health—medical history, labs, and what’s most bothersome day-to-day. From there, we talk through options together. I like to start low, follow up closely, and adjust thoughtfully to make sure treatments are working well and improving their quality of life."
