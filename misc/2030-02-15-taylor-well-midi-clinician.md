Taylor Well, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Experience in whole-person care: "Over my years in healthcare, I have delivered patient-centered care across multiple diverse settings; urgent care, family practice, in-home care, and preventative medicine. I’ve managed both acute and chronic conditions for patients of all genders and ages, but I have always had a passion for supporting women."

Hearing it firsthand: "When I follow up with a patient and they say, ‘I'm feeling so much better because of the treatment plan we decided on together,’ it reminds me why I do this. I highly value the patient-provider partnership and sharing decision making."

Empowering confidence: "My philosophy is simple: I want my patients to feel heard, supported, and confident in their health decisions. My goal has always been to care for my patients like I would want someone to care for me, my family, my friends, and my community."

A trusting conversation: "My approach is all about building a trusting relationship with my patients.
