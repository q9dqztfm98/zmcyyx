The 33-year-old actor is producing his own projects like The Valiant One on Hulu and is an ambassador at large for Bring Change to Mind, a non-profit that works to normalize mental health conversations among teens. And, when the surf’s up, he’s riding waves near his home in Los Angeles. Here’s the gear that keeps him in flow.
