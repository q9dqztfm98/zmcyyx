What to Consider

Cushioning Technology

New Balance uses two main cushioning platforms: Fresh Foam X for plush comfort and smoother transitions, and FuelCell for responsive energy return. Your choice depends on whether you prefer cloud-like softness (great for recovery or all-day wear) or springy propulsion (ideal for commuters and fast-paced walkers).

Upper Construction

New Balance is known for its breathable engineered mesh uppers in performance models and suede/mesh blends in lifestyle classics like the 574. Its uppers are often reinforced with strategic overlays to balance flexibility with structure, so you can expect a snug, supportive feel without sacrificing airflow.

Design

Many New Balance models incorporate rocker geometries to encourage forward momentum during walking, while others focus on flat, grippy outsoles for stability. Depending on whether you’re walking city streets, light trails, or spending hours on hard floors, the outsole design can make a huge difference in comfort and durability.

Function

Think about how and where you’ll be wearing the shoes. Do you need something for long shifts on your feet? City walking? Easy hikes? The best walking shoes aren’t always the flashiest—they’re the ones that match your day-to-day lifestyle and feel just as good in hour five as they did in hour one.

New Balance is also one of the rare brands that consistently offers wide sizing across most of its models. That means if you need a roomier toe box or a tailored fit, NB likely has you covered. Think about whether you need a shoe for all-day standing, extended walking tours, or versatile wear that can flex between casual outings and light workouts—the function should align with your daily lifestyle.

Are Running Shoes Good For Walking?

This is a common question I get from my clients and friends—and generally the answer is yes. However, there may be certain running shoes that may not be the best for walking. There are running shoes that are geared for performance such as racing shoes, max cushion shoes, or even minimalistic running shoes.

If you are looking for a versatile option, I would suggest going for a daily trainer that is designed to be durable to handle a lot of steps while also offering a decent amount of cushion. Getting a “super shoe” or plated shoe to walk and occasionally run in because it looks sporty could be costly and quite simply—unnecessary.
