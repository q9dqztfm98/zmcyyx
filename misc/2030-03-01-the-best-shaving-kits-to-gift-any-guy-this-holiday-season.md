Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

LEARNING HOW to shave is a right of passage for any young men, but learning how to shave well takes years of trial and error. Getting a close, comfortable shave at home requires quite a few products, including shave oil, shaving cream, aftershave, and a good razor. It's easy to get overwhelmed by the amount of products and steps that it takes to get a decent shave from the comfort of your home. Whether you’re the kind of guy whose entire routine takes five minutes or you can spend the better part of an hour in front of the mirror, creating a shaving routine can be a lot of work.

That’s why we love shaving kits—they can take the guesswork out of the complicated shaving process. Instead of walking around the store with a basket full of products, shaving sets offer the chance to get everything you need in one place (bonus points if they also include a razor). Sure, most shaving kits are designed as gifts for men, but we say gift them to yourself first. They’re the perfect chance to try new products, discover new brands, and maybe even improve the effectiveness of your shave so you can get a clean, close shave in half the time.
