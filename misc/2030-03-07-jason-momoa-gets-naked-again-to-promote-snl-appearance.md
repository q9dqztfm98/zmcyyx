Jason Momoa is all kinds of excited to return to Saturday Night Live for his second stint as celebrity guest host. In a new promo video, the Aquaman star strides into Studio 8H, the longstanding home of the show, amid a montage of memories from his previous appearances.

Unable to contain himself, the famously irrepressible Momoa then proceeds to bound and leap around the soundstage with gleeful abandon... until he is interrupted by regular cast member Ego Nwodim, who points out that he is almost naked from the waist down.

"I'm sorry," he says. "When I get excited I forget my pants."

He then refuses to get dressed and continues to frolic, throwing himself at cast member Marcello Hernandez, who accepts his pantsless embrace.

It's certainly one way to get audiences ready for Momoa's episode this weekend.

Momoa has proven himself to be a good sport on SNL before, playing up his larger-than-life persona with characters like Apollo Benz, the paralegal-turned-gigolo. And he is hardly shy when it comes to showing off his body on TV: he spent most of his time on Game of Thrones shirtless, and frequently posts scantily-clad content to his Instagram, even sharing a video of himself taking a fully nude bubblebath.
