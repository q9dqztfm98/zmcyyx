# Keegan Bradley Addresses His ‘Full Swing’ Rejection

THE SECOND SEASON of Netflix's Full Swing is now streaming, and follows several professional golfers as they play their best in the hopes of qualifying for the U.S. team in the 2023 Ryder Cup in Rome. It became clear that several PGA players were tight on and off the green, with Justin "JT" Thomas, Jordan Spieth and Rickie Fowler forming a close unit—but outlier Keegan Bradley has become something of a fan favorite among viewers.

[Keegan Bradley](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/9481607779.html)

This was never more so than in the sixth episode of the season, "Pick Six," in which Bradley wins the Travelers Championship and climbs the points system, making it look like he might be about to play his first Ryder Cup in almost 10 years, while also opening up his home to the cameras and sharing what this dream means to him and his family.

But while Bradley came across as a skilled and highly likeable athlete, he appeared to be at a disadvantage when it came to who team captain Zach Johnson would choose to take to Rome.

"Pick Six" showed that the close-knit crew of JT, Spieth and Fowler also shared a friendship with Johnson, with the four guys sharing a house at the British Open. The episode also included footage of the four guys at dinner, where Johnson was jokingly questioned on who he was selecting. By contrast, Bradley spent his free time at the Open alone, calling his wife from a hotel room. And by the end of the episode, Johnson had called Bradley to break the news to him that he would not be playing in the Ryder Cup.

Now that the show is out there and he's had a chance to watch, Bradley recognizes that a kind of PGA "boys' club" formed without him, which affected his chances of selection—but he remains sportsmanlike in retrospect.

"It was interesting to see for sure," he recently told Sports Illustrated. "Obviously I sort of do my own thing out there, but I knew I was behind the 8 ball for sure in that category. I said to myself, if you just keep playing well and winning tournaments, that’s the only way I can prove myself. I love those guys—JT and Rickie and Jordan—so much. I hate saying a negative word or thinking even a negative thought about them."

Bradley admits that Thomas, Spieth and Fowler's friendship made him a little "jealous," if only "because it makes their life on Tour that much more fun," but that he was also pragmatic about the outcome going into that situation.

"But I kept telling myself, listen, this is not going to be easy," he continued. "You’re not going to be picked if you’re a fringe pick. You have to go prove that they have to pick you. When I didn’t make the team on points, I knew that was a big risk. I knew that if it was down to me or some of those guys, I probably wouldn’t get picked. I knew that going in and I just kept trying to play as hard as I could."
