Who was Roscoe Conkling?

Roscoe Conkling was an American politician, born in 1829. Played by Shea Whigham in Death by Lightning, he represented New York in both the House of Representatives and the Senate. He held a lot of power in the state, predominantly through his control of the New York Customs House. This meant he also had a significant impact in dealing with various presidents, whether it was a positive relationship with Ulysses S. Grant, or striking up feuds with Rutherford B. Hayes and James A. Garfield.

Why did Roscoe Conkling not like James Garfield?

Conkling is shown throughout Death by Lightning as the antagonist in Garfield’s political career. During the presidential election of 1880, Garfield thought John Sherman was the best pick for the nomination.

According to Vox, the Republican party at the time was divided into two groups. There were the “Half-Breeds” seeking civil reform, and the “Stalwarts.” Conkling was the floor leader for the latter cluster, and he supported former President Ulysses S. Grant—hoping that the other delegates would do the same. However, although Garfield denied wanting to be nominated, a few Republicans put his name forward.

What happened to Roscoe Conkling?

Angry enough to step down from his position as a Senator of New York, Conkling’s move to resign ultimately ended his political career. He returned to practicing law, and became a governing member of the New York Athletic Club.

Conkling contracted pneumonia in March of 1888 after deciding to walk home three miles during the Great Blizzard of 1888, according to WNYC. It progressed to meningitis, and he died on April 18, 1888.
