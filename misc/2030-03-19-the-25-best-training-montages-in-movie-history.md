# The 25 Best Training Montages in Movie History

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

For as long as characters have needed to get whipped into shape over the course of a minute or two, there's been the training montage. Here, we look back at the 25 best of all time, from a swamp planet to imperial China, with a stop at the top of the Philadelphia Museum of Art along the way.

Karate Kid (1984)

What's probably the most-quoted training montage in history is also probably the least physical, because you don't learn karate to fight, you learn so you don't have to fight. Wax on, wax off.

Rocky (1976)

The training montage that made training montages a "thing." Small-time Philly brawler Rocky Balboa makes it to the top of the steps.

Cool Runnings (1993)

Feel both the rhythm and the rhyme of undisputedly the best training montage featuring a Jamaican bobsled team.

The Empire Strikes Back (1980)

The Star Wars saga's most educational moment has taught generations of fitness enthusiasts that there is only "do" or "do not." There is no "try."

Rocky II (1979)

While it doesn't quite top the iconic montage from the original Rocky, it is worth pointing out that Balboa actually wins the fight he's training for here.

The Fighter (2010)

In The Fighter, Mark Wahlberg perfectly captures the struggle of trying to cut weight by working out in sweatpants.

Batman Begins (2005)

Nothing has ever made more sense than the person who taught Batman to be an unstoppable badass turning out to be Liam Neeson.

Rocky III (1982)

Sure, pre-workout is fine, but have you ever tried just watching extremely jacked Sylvester Stallone and Carl Weathers run down the beach to "Eye of the Tiger?"

Bloodsport (1988)

This six-minute long montage remains legendary for basically introducing the world to what Jean-Claude Van Damme does best (see: kick very hard and do crazy low splits).

Mulan (1998)

Sorry Hercules, but Mulan is the most yoked 90's Disney character of all time. Don't @ me.

No Retreat, No Surrender (1986)

No Retreat, No Surrender is an absolutely batshit film — featuring the ghost of Bruce Lee and Jean-Claude Van Damme playing a Soviet villain — that comes with an equally insane training montage.

Rocky IV (1985)

The best training montage of all time, in this writer's humble opinion: Rocky goes as old-school as possible in the snowy Russian wilderness as Dolph Lundgren's Ivan Drago is practically built in a laboratory using steroids and machines.

Footloose (1984)

Kevin Bacon aggressively dancing his soul out (and...performing gymnastics) in an empty warehouse is the absolute peak of 1980s physicality...

Hot Rod (2007)

...and it also inspired this very, very spot-on scene from Hot Rod.

Team America: World Police (2004)

Trey Parker and Matt Stone's "Montage" from Team America: World Police pretty much speaks for itself (and this whole list, actually): "And with every shot you show a little improvement / To show it all would take too long / That's called a montage."

The Incredibles (2004)

Pixar's The Incredibles may be animated, but dang if a superhero struggling to shred inches off his waistline isn't relatable.

Rocky Balboa (2006)

The then-60-year-old Sylvester Stallone he could still pull off a hell of a montage 30 years after the first Rocky, raw eggs and all.

Enough (2002)

Enough overall is a forgettable 2002 movie, but watching Jennifer Lopez learn to kick ass is a gem all its own.

G.I. Jane (1997)

Demi Moore whipped herself into actual NAVY Seal shape for G.I. Jane, training she puts to good work with a few one-armed pushups.
