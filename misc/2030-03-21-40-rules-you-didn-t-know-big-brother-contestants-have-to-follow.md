# 40 Rules You Didn't Know 'Big Brother' Contestants Have to Follow

For 22 seasons, we've watched in awe and entertainment as a group of strangers live in total isolation together and compete for a $500,000 cash prize on Big Brother. CBS's reality competition show is full of rules and regulations, and we've rounded up the most outrageous and surprising just for you. As the show films an all-star season amidst the COVID-19 pandemic, a few more rules have been implemented—find out more here.

They have to undergo a psychological evaluation.

Before being seriously considered for the show, those who are hoping to be a houseguest must undergo a psychological evaluation and an IQ test. After the tests, the finalist pool is whittled down by half.

They have to pass an in-person interview.

[Know Big Brother'](https://yldzdh.com/akpnbgff4p/)

According to Reality Blurred, finalists are flown to Los Angeles for an in-person interview. After this round, they either make it into the house or are sent home.

They have to get tested for STDs.

Although the show's producers don't encourage sexual relationships between houseguests, they know it happens from time to time. To protect contestants from spreading sexually transmitted diseases in the Big Brother house, everyone is tested beforehand.

Being outgoing and adaptable will help you get cast.

There's a certain personality that flourishes on Big Brother. While searching for cast members, producers look for individuals who are "strong willed, outgoing, adventurous, physically and mentally adept, adaptable to new environments, and to have interesting life styles, backgrounds and personalities," according to Reality Blurred.

They have to be ready to start filming on short notice.

A perspective finalist on Big Brother told Reality Blurred that finalists are contacted a week before the camera crews head to the house. According to the contract, they could be notified only 24 hours ahead of time.

They have to have watched at least one season of the show.

Big Brother isn't your typical reality show—it's a competition and producers want to know that prospective houseguests are well versed in the rules of the show and have developed their own strategy to win.

They have to sign an extensive contract.

Every contestant on the show signs a contract that gives producers the right to portray them, their story, and their image in any way they see fit. Essentially, the houseguests forfeit any control they have over their portrayal on TV before they even step foot in the house.

They have to pose for the Memory Wall.

Another rule before entering the house: Each houseguest has to pose for a portrait for the Memory Wall, which helps contestants keep track of current houseguests. After someone is eliminated, their photo fades to black and white, signaling their eviction.

They have to quarantine before entering the house.

Season 22 of Big Brother is being filmed during the COVID-19 pandemic, so the show has taken extra safety precautions, including mandatory quarantines before entering the house. Production set up accommodations for the cast to self-isolate in rental homes. "If you ask me, once you're in the Big Brother house, it might be one of the safest places to be," host Julie Chen told Entertainment Tonight.

They have to take COVID-19 tests regularly.

To ensure the houseguests are free and clear of the virus, the show is testing them throughout their stay. "They're getting tested every few days. They will go in once they've been clean and clear for at least 14 days and then even once they get in the house, they're still going to be tested," Chen told Entertainment Tonight.

They're given the show's rulebook upon arrival.

When the contestants first enter the Big Brother house, they're each given a copy of the show's rule books. These rulebooks, as per their contract, are subject to change—with no warning—at the hands of the producers.

They have to agree to being searched.

According to the show's contract, there's a list of approved items each houseguest can bring into the house and any prohibited items are confiscated by production before contestants even step foot inside the house.

They can't bring their phones.

Along with their phones, contestants can't use their email or access the internet during their stay. This ensures that houseguests are as isolated from the outside world as possible—a key part of the show.

They have to cut off all outside contact.

The cast must remain completely sequestered while filming, which means cast members are prohibited from being in contact with friends or family.

They can't leave the house.

Once houseguests arrive at the Big Brother house, if they want to continue participating in the competition, they can't leave the house, unless they're evicted.

They can't shop for their own groceries.

Since the houseguests aren't allowed to leave the house, production organizes the house's groceries to be delivered each week. This practice will continue in season 22, with the additional step of the groceries being disinfected for the houseguests.

They have to agree to eating slop.

[Contestants Have](https://bioresearch.wiki/pqk51hr/)

On Big Brother, one punishment houseguests can receive is being forced to eat slop after losing a competition. The oatmeal-like food made its first appearance in season 7 and has continued to be a staple on the show.

They can't whisper in the house.

Although it seems like a hard thing to enforce, houseguests are forbidden from whispering while filming in the house, because it's hard to capture this audio on microphones.

Their interactions with producers are restricted.

[Contestants Have to](https://ppmaas.com/bz0w80skuv/)

During a normal season, the producers have limited access to the houseguests and are only allowed to speak to one another in the Diary Room. For season 22, producers are taking extra precautions. "We produce this behind the walls. They only hear our voices. They don't really see us throughout their stay in the Big Brother house," executive producer, Allison Grodner told Entertainment Tonight.

They can't discuss nominations with other housemates.

Each week, houseguests are selected to nominate their fellow contestants for eviction. After being selected to nominate that week, the contestant has to do so privately and cannot discuss their choices with anyone beforehand.
