# The Best Christmas Pop Songs of All Time

Decorative trees, gift-giving, all out brawls at the dinner table—sound familiar? But who could forget another iconic tradition associated with this celebratory time of year: Christmas music. Not only does a great Christmas pop song set the mood, it also makes you excited about the holiday season, even if it entails a six-hour flight, a crowded airport, or staying at home on your own on the 25th (which, in pandemic times, is likely more common than ever).

From classics songs by '80s pop duo Wham! to the extremely overplayed but still very enjoyable ones by Mariah Carey, Christmas pop is a subgenre on its own and a damn good one at that. Below, 25 of the best Christmas pop songs of all time to get you in the holiday spirit. (Our gift to you.)

"All I Want For Christmas Is You" by Mariah Carey

It's been 26 years since this legendary song debuted on the airwaves. Still, it remains a classic to this day (and a chart-topper every December). If this doesn't get you in the seasonal spirit, check your pulse now.

"The Christmas Song" by Shawn Mendes and Camila Cabello

Real-life couple Shawn Mendes and former Fifth Harmony member Camila Cabello give their own spin on a holiday classic. It's a quiet, sweet song that feels just like those first few moments waking up on Christmas day.

"Snow in California" by Ariana Grande

[The Best Christmas Pop](https://healingkinetics.bbsxyxy.com/)

Ariana Grande’s Christmas songs are, rather inexplicably, one of the best parts of her discography. This standout single from her 2013 EP. “Christmas Kisses,” is the gift that keeps on giving.

"Santa Tell Me" by Ariana Grande

Another iconic Christmas classic by Ariana Grande that isn't overtly sentimental, if you tend to fall into the camp that hates weepy holiday music.

"Last Christmas" by Wham!

[Best Christmas Pop](https://ppmaas.com/x5ulz8l/)

This tune about holiday heartbreak has been covered by many, but nothing can beat the OG song. It might only be October but this song can make you feel like it's snowing outside.

“Happy Xmas (War is Over) by Miley Cyrus and Mark Ronson ft. Sean Ono Lennon

Cyrus, accompanied by Ono Lennon, covers this Christmas classic that also doubles as a protest song, originally performed by Lennon's parents. With themes revolving around peace and harmony, there’s never been a more fitting song to close out 2020.

"Winter Song" by Ingrid Michaelson and Sara Bareilles

Sometimes, you do need a good cry this time of year. (No judgment.) Let this quiet, but beautifully sung holiday tune be your soundtrack.

"Ho Ho Ho" by Sia

If you couldn't tell by now, most Christmas songs fall into two camps: the sad and the cheesy. But this track of Sia's 2018 Christmas album is actually just plain fun and probably the song you need in this exact moment.

“Mistletoe” by Justin Bieber

Let this 2011 Christmas hit transport you to a time where you didn’t have to stand six feet apart from your holiday crush (and a time when Bieber's bangs were all the rage).

"Baby, It's Cold Outside" by John Legend and Kelly Clarkson

[of All](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

Two powerhouse singers and Voice coaches crooning a classic, sans the creepy lyrics. ("It's your body and your choice.") We stan.

"Santa Baby" by Eartha Kitt

This classic song by Eartha Kitt has been often imitated, but never duplicated. It's Eartha Kitt, after all.

“Christmas in Harlem” by Kanye West ft. Teyana Taylor and Cyhi the Prince

It’s not quite a pop song, but this 2010 song by West is an underrated holiday hit.

"Blackout Christmas" by Vérité

Brooklyn-based singer-songwriter Vérité delivers one hell of a Christmas tune with lyrics about accidentally getting too drunk on the special day to get over heartbreak.

"8 Days of Christmas” by Destiny’s Child

The trio’s take on “12 Days of Christmas” is simply superior to the original. Who the hells wants a bird in a tree when you can have diamond jewelry, sings Beyonce?

"Christmas Makes Me Cry” by Kacey Musgraves

The pop-country queen wrote this masterpiece that perfectly encapsulates the holiday blues. Who needs joy and holiday cheer when you can sulk for the entirety of December?

"Christmases When You Were Mine” by Taylor Swift

This song about being alone during the holidays has never been more fitting for 2020. And while you're at it, might as well stream the entirety of Swift's Folklore, a perfect album for sorting through those holiday blues.

“Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas” by Sam Smith

Smith’s 2014 cover of the classic Christmas gets us every time. Their velvety vocals elicit an emotion that can only be drawn out by the voice of a real-life angel.

"Underneath the Tree” by Kelly Clarkson

Clarkson’s original Christmas song reminds us of the true importance of the holidays: gifts. (And love and stuff.)

“This Christmas” by Christina Aguilera

If there’s one thing you can be sure of, it’s that Christina will always deliver powerful vocals, particularly in one of the most iconic Christmas songs of them all.

“Snowflakes of Love” by Toni Braxton

Listen to Braxton's voice just a few lines in and you'll realize why this tops our list. A holiday classic in the making.
