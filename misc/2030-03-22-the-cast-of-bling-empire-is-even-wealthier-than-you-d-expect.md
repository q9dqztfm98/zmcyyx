# The Cast of 'Bling Empire' Is Even Wealthier Than You'd Expect

[Even Wealthier Than You'd](https://peplawllc.com/blog/8506165515.html)

There are a lot of words you can use to describe the cast of Netflix's latest reality show, Bling Empire—dramatic, crazy, and over the top, to name a few—but who could forget this one: really insanely rich. Capturing a group of wealthy Asian-Americans residing in L.A, the show follows the cast as they openly flaunt the latest fashions and drop a few thousand dollars on a whim. A spur-of-the-moment trip to Paris? Sure thing! Expensive jewelry? No problem!

While Netflix viewers are fascinated by just how much drama the cast can conjure up, they're also likely wondering just how much money the cast is worth. Given their expensive homes, lavish parties, and love of designer brands, it makes sense that they'd have a good amount of money stored in their bank accounts. Here's a quick look at just how much some of the main cast members are estimated to be worth, based on information that's been made public—and where their money comes from.

Kane Lim

Net worth: at least $1 million

While his parents are wealthy billionaires on their own, Lim is not just using family money for personal expenses. He started making his own cash as a stock investor, and he does have his hand in a series of real estate properties. If you're curious of how much his extensive shoe collection is worth, Women's Wear Daily reported it to be around $300,000. While websites claim he's made around $20 million, we don't have much evidence for it. In a 2019 interview, he did, however, claim he'd made at least seven figures before he entered his 20s, calling the sum "small."
