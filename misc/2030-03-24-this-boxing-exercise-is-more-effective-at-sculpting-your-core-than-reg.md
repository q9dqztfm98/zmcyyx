# This Boxing Exercise Is More Effective At Sculpting Your Core Than Regular Crunches

If you want to get the most out of your core, skip out on regular crunches.

Crunches tend to be the default when it comes to building up abs. And though they do help target your abdominal muscles, the fact of the matter is that they aren’t the most effective move you can be doing for your core. That’s where Nike trainer Dennys Lozada comes in.

“One of the best core exercises you’re possibly not doing is a boxer's in-and-out under the ropes,” says Lozada, an instructor at Everybody Fights in New York.

In the video above, he uses a boxing ring to train his abs. But if you don’t have a ring, try to just imagine having a rope directly over your head, Lozada says.

First grab a set of weights. Get into position by lying down on the ring directly underneath one of the ropes with your heels down and knees up. Keep the weights pressed to your chest the whole time.

As you come up on one side, contract your obliques and core, never allowing your shoulder blades to touch the floor. Go under the rope and repeat the move on the other side.

By working in different planes of motion and incorporating weights, you’re actually targeting and strengthening additional muscles all while still working on your abs.

Lozada suggests performing the exercise for 30 to 45 seconds with 10 to 15 seconds off for three rounds. “That’ll really fire your core up,” he says.

Make sure to focus on maintaining a proper form rather than cranking out as many crunches as possible for the best results.
