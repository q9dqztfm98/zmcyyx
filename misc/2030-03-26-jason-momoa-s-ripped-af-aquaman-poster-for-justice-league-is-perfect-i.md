Jason Momoa was probably born huge, just popped right out on day one and immediately started doing pullups on the side of his mother's gurney, but he's in truly rare form for Justice League. A new poster of Momoa in full costume (well, full shirtless costume) started circulating on the internet yesterday, and yup, he's shredded.

Momoa plays Aquaman, one of the five founding members of the Justice League, in the DC Universe's first big team-up movie. Fortunately, he didn't have to change his look very much — he's basically just Khal Drogo with more ink and swimming. What's cool is that the costume designers seem to have incorporated Momoa's real-life looks into the character — Aquaman's full chest and arm tattoos actually sync up with a real tattoo Momoa has on his left forearm. Most of the ink is fake, of course, (it looks like the makeup squad gave his kids some tats for a day, too), but the total effect is pretty bad-ass.
