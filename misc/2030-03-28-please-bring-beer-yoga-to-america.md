# Please Bring Beer Yoga To America

Yoga may have a reputation for being a predominantly female form of exercise and meditation, but lots of men swear by the practice to break a serious sweat and forge a mind-body connection—including special ops soldiers. So yoga is already plenty acceptable for guys. But you know what would make it even more dude-worthy? Adding beer to the mix.

Enter the German geniuses who came up with BierYoga—which is, quite simply, the “marriage of two great loves: beer and yoga,” according to the organization’s website. “Both are centuries-old therapies for mind, body, and soul,” the founders say. Now Aussies are coming around to the idea, holding two special BierYoga sessions in Sydney this weekend. Here’s how it works: Over the course of an hour, you drink a bottle or two of beer while striking yoga poses at the same time. Students will learn poses with “beer salutations” and balance beer bottles on their head, according to Broadsheet Sydney.
