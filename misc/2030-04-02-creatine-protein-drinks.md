Q: If I make my post-workout protein and creatine shake before my workout, and keep it in the fridge, will it still be okay after my workout?

Answer:Yes, it won't be a big deal at all.

Creatine breaks down in liquids, but at a relatively slow rate. You could leave it in the fridge over night and you'd still have 90% of the creatine in the drink, so no need to stress about this.
