# 3 Lessons from Tiger Woods

First of all, Tiger Woods is not on steroids. Anyone who thinks he is knows nothing about the ability of a young man to adapt to a good exercise routine. In fact, we can learn a lot from Tiger thanks to devotion to his incredible physical transformation over the years.

Here are the 3 biggest lessons you can learn from Tiger Woods...

1) Use a variety of bodyweight and free weight exercises to work on your strength and mobility. By doing so, Tiger has been able to maintain his mobility and flexibility while adding strength and muscle.
