# These Tough-as-Hell Marines Are Running Through Combat Drills in the Snow

U.S. and South Korean Marines have been getting into the Olympic spirit the past few weeks, going through their annual winter training exercises together in Pyeongchang, which will play host to the Winter Olympics set to begin in February of next year. Only instead of uniforms, the guys are running through many of the snowy drills shirtless.

According to Yonhap News Agency, South Korean and U.S. Marines have been teaming up since 2013 for an annual three week-long winter training session in the South Korean city. The two sides join together to "sharpen their combat skills in subfreezing temperatures." Pictures of the scene show that while there's certainly a shortage of shirts, there's no lack of neither subfreezing temperatures nor combat skills. Obviously, there is no excuse for letting fitness slip in the winter.
