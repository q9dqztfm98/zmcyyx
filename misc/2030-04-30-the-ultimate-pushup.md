# The Ultimate Pushup

If you want to get more on the floor, try a classic exercise called the mechanical change pushup. You start in the hardest pushup position (hands under shoulders), and as you become fatigued, you adjust your hand-width—making the exercise a little easier—so that you can extend your set. The upshot: You do more work with your chest, triceps, and shoulders. In fact, it's guaranteed to make your muscles pumped.
