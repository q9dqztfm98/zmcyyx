Obesity prevention centers on two evidence-based habits: balanced nutrition and consistent physical activity.

Healthy eating: Prioritize whole foods such as vegetables, fruits, lean proteins, whole grains, and healthy fats. Limit ultra-processed foods, added sugars, and sugary beverages. Practice portion control and mindful eating.

Regular physical activity: Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week (e.g., brisk walking, cycling, swimming), plus muscle-strengthening activities twice weekly.

Additional factors—quality sleep, stress management, and hydration—also play a critical role in maintaining a healthy weight.

Preventing obesity involves a combination of healthy eating and regular exercise. Eating a balanced diet with plenty of fruits and vegetables while limiting sugary and high-fat foods is important.

Here are some tips to help prevent obesity:

Healthy Eating: Choose foods that are low in sugar and fat. Eat lots of fruits, vegetables, and whole grains.

Regular Exercise: Be active every day. This can include walking, playing sports, or even dancing.
