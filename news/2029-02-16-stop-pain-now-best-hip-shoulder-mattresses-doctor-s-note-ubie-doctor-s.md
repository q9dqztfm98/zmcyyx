Explanation

Hip and shoulder pain can make a good night's sleep feel impossible, especially for side sleepers. Choosing the right mattress is crucial to relieve pressure points and maintain proper spinal alignment. Below, we explore the factors side sleepers should consider and highlight top mattress options for hip and shoulder pain relief.

Why Mattress Choice Matters for Side Sleepers

Side sleepers place most of their body weight on the hips and shoulders. Without adequate cushioning and support, pressure builds up, leading to:

Joint aches and stiffness

Numbness or tingling in arms or legs

Lower back pain from poor spinal alignment

A mattress designed for pressure relief can distribute weight evenly, reduce stress on sensitive areas, and promote a neutral spine position.

Key Features to Look For

When hunting for the best mattress for side sleepers, focus on these core features:

Memory foam or latex layers that conform to curves

Zoned foam to target hips and shoulders

Medium to medium-soft firmness (generally a 4–6 on a 1–10 scale)

Durable base layer to prevent excessive sinkage

Contouring that keeps spine straight from neck to hips

Edge support to prevent sagging at the sides

Cooling gel-infused foam or breathable latex

Hybrid designs with coils for airflow

Minimizes partner disturbance if you share the bed
