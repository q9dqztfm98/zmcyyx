Don’t Burn Your Toast? Charred toast doesn’t just ruin your breakfast—it might hurt your health, too. The Food Standards Agency in the U.K. is launching a campaign called “Go For Gold,” which encourages people to shoot for a gold, rather than dark brown color, when frying, baking, or toasting foods like potatoes, root vegetables, and bread. That’s because the organization says the high temperatures can increase the amount of a compound called acrylamide in the food. And that can be a problem, since acrylamidecause has been linked to cancer. But, the link hasn’t been proven in humans, the BBC reports.
