What to Consider

Hair Thickening Product vs. Hair Loss Treatment

Granted, you’ll find plenty of products out there that combine both categories (like many on this list), however the general rule of thumb is that thickening products provide superficial thickness to hair to make it look fuller. These are usually styling products like powders, sprays and creams. On the flip side, hair loss treatments address the underlying cause of hair loss, which thins our hair. These include minoxidil foams, serums, LED tools and more. Depending on what you’re hoping to achieve, you may choose one or the other, but if you want both short and long term results, we suggest using both.

Do Hair Thickening Products Work?

Like all grooming products, not everything is created equal. Most hair thickening products work as long as you use them properly. The most important thing to remember when using any sort of hair thickening treatment, especially those that target the underlying causes of hair loss, is that you use them consistently. Unless otherwise noted, most of these products are meant to be used at least once a day and the general rule of thumb is that they take three to six months for results to start showing.

Other thickening products, like styling products, can give an instant (yet temporary) look of fullness to your hair. Many of these use ingredients like polymers to thicken the hair shafts themselves or others like sea salt or powder to create texture within the hair to make it appear fuller. These, unlike treatment products, are temporary and the results can diminish over time unless you reapply or restyle your hair.
