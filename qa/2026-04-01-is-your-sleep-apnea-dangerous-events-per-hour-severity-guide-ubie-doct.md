What does the number of sleep apnea events per hour indicate about severity?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sleep apnea severity is measured by the Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI), which counts the number of breathing interruptions per hour of sleep. Mild sleep apnea involves 5–14 events per hour, moderate ranges from 15–29 events, and severe sleep apnea is 30 or more events per hour. A higher AHI means more disrupted breathing, greater oxygen deprivation, and increased risk of health complications like heart disease, stroke, and daytime fatigue.
