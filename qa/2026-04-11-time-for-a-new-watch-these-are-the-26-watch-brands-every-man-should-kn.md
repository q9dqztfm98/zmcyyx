Rolex

Founded in: 1905 | Average price: $5,000-$400,000 | Made in: Switzerland

Arguably the most well-known watch brand, Rolex is synonymous with the industry for a reason: they make high-quality pieces that last forever, and in some cases even appreciate in value over time. IWC Schaffhausen

Founded in: 1868 | Average price: $4,250-$56,700 | Made in: Switzerland

The International Watch Company is often referred to either as the Swiss watch brand founded by an American or as the pilot watch brand. Really, IWC's Mark XVIII has pretty much become the prototype for aviation watches: huge numerals, a big crown, and a triangle at 12. The brand also uses top-of-the-line materials like titanium. And its models like Da Vinci and Portofino will blow you away with movement and designs you've probably never seen of.

Bulova

Founded in: 1875 | Average price: $250-$4,500 | Made in: Switzerland

The most famous artifact in Bulova's catalog is perhaps the Lunar Pilot, brought to the surface of the moon in 1971 during the Apollo 15 mission. But digging within the brand's treasure trove, you can also find a slew of beautiful dress watches and chronographs with sophisticated openwork designs. And its Precisionist model will completely sweep you off your feet with its details and quartz movement.

Longines

Founded in: 1832 | Average price: $800-$12,000 | Made in: Switzerland

Longines is one of the few watchmakers with heritage and specialty in just about every type of timepiece, thanks to the brand's history in innovation, though dive watches, dress watches, and aviation watches are where it takes the limelight. Its Spirit pilot model is rather legendary and always under the radar thanks to its reasonable price and handsome designs that won't go out of style.

Vacheron Constantin

Founded in: 1755 | Average price: $12,000-$131,000 | Made in: Switzerland

The first word that comes to mind when seeing a Vacheron watch is timeless. The brand's designs are meant to be passed down from generation to generation and always feel modern. Established in 1755, the brand has one of the most expansive watchmaking reputations in the game and is a who's who for watch collectors and insiders alike.

Patek Philippe

Founded in: 1839 | Average price: $15,000-$450,000 | Made in: Switzerland

Another heavyweight in the luxury watch industry is Patek. As one of the only fully family-owned and operated watch businesses in existence today, Patek has the full freedom to design, manufacture, and assemble what many experts deem the finest watches in the world. Hublot watches are some of the most high-tech-looking pieces on the market.
