What are the benefits of getting a house call doctor for preop clearance?

House call doctors can make pre-op clearance much easier, especially in the new normal. The house call doctor can coordinate with your surgeons and other relevant healthcare providers to ensure that it’s safe for you to move forward with the procedure – all without you needing to leave home.

Given that negative Covid-19 tests are now a standard procedure before surgeries, getting pre-op clearance at home as a one-time service helps reduce the chance that you’ll be exposed to Covid-19 or its variants before your surgery.

Should your house call doctor be your primary physician?

If you have a chronic illness or condition that requires numerous procedures or surgeries, and you haven’t found a PCP you’re comfortable with, you may want to consider switching to a house call doctor. Mobile concierge providers typically have much more time to meet with patients than PCPs who struggle with massive administrative duties and costs associated with insurance companies.

The average appointment time with a house call doctor is 30-45 minutes – three times what standard care allows per patient. Additionally, many practices supplement in-home appointments with telehealth, meaning health concerns can be addressed as they come up, rather than having to schedule appointments far in advance for a simple question. (Insurance companies require doctors to meet with patients in order to get reimbursed, which is why doctors are forced to bring patients into the office unnecessarily.)

This extra time can make a big difference in patient outcomes for a number of reasons. First, longer appointments mean forming better relationships with your doctor, meaning you’ll feel more comfortable asking questions as you build a rapport. This typically results in health issues getting addressed early, before they can become serious and expensive conditions.
