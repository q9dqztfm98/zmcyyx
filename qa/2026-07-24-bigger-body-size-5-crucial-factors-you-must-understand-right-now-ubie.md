Larger body size, or obesity, develops when several factors interact: fat tissue expansion, an increased tendency to gain weight, and psychological or behavioral influences such as eating patterns, stress, and emotional health. Genetics, hormones, metabolism, sleep, and lifestyle habits also play key roles in how the body stores and regulates fat.

Because body size changes can stem from many overlapping causes, understanding what's driving yours is the first step toward meaningful action. Nelson and colleagues highlight that psychological and behavioral factors are important too. This includes how people feel or behave, which can affect eating habits and activity levels.
