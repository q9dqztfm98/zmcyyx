Can left-sided pain under the ribs be linked to spleen or kidney problems?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Pain on the left side under the ribs can signal issues with the spleen or left kidney, including splenic infarction, splenic torsion, kidney infections, or kidney stones. Other possible causes include pancreatitis, gastritis, muscle strain, or cardiac-related conditions. Because the symptoms can overlap across serious and minor conditions, self-diagnosing is difficult and often inaccurate.

Kidney Problems: Kidney issues like infarctions, which are blockages that cut off blood flow, can also cause pain in this area. Infarctions can affect both the spleen and kidneys, often linked to conditions like atrial fibrillation, which is a type of irregular heartbeat.
