# Are Long-Term Sleeping Pills Safe for Seniors? Urgent Warning! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Are long-term sleeping pills safe for seniors?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Long-term sleeping pill use in seniors carries serious risks, including falls, fractures, memory problems, confusion, daytime drowsiness, and physical dependency. Older adults metabolize medications more slowly, which can intensify side effects and interact dangerously with other prescriptions. Safer alternatives exist—such as cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), improved sleep hygiene, and treating underlying conditions like sleep apnea or anxiety. Seniors should speak with their doctor before starting or continuing sleep medications long-term.
