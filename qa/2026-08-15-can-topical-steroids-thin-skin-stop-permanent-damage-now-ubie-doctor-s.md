Can topical steroids thin the skin?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, topical steroids can thin the skin, particularly with prolonged or high-potency use. This side effect, known as skin atrophy, occurs because steroids slow down collagen production and reduce skin cell turnover. Thinner skin may appear fragile, translucent, or bruise more easily, and blood vessels underneath can become more visible. The risk increases with stronger steroids, longer treatment duration, and application to sensitive areas like the face, groin, or skin folds.
