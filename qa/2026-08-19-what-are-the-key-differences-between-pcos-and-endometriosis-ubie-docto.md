Explanation

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and endometriosis are both conditions that affect women, but they are quite different in how they present and affect the body.

PCOS:

PCOS is a hormonal disorder that often leads to irregular periods, excess hair growth, and sometimes cysts on the ovaries.

[PCOS and](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/blog/7900197989.html)

It is associated with higher levels of male hormones, which can cause symptoms like acne and weight gain.

Endometriosis:

Endometriosis involves the growth of tissue similar to the lining inside the uterus, but it grows outside the uterus.

It can cause painful periods, pain during intercourse, and sometimes fertility issues.

Both conditions can affect fertility, but they do so in different ways. PCOS is more about hormonal imbalances, while endometriosis is about tissue growing where it shouldn’t. Understanding these differences can help in managing symptoms and seeking the right treatment.
