Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Wegovy (semaglutide) is an FDA-approved weight-loss medication that works by activating GLP-1 receptors. Clinical trials have not demonstrated that Wegovy directly shrinks uterine fibroids or relieves fibroid-related pain.

However, weight loss achieved with Wegovy may indirectly ease fibroid symptoms. Reduced adipose tissue can lower circulating estrogen levels, decrease systemic inflammation, and reduce pelvic pressure — all of which may contribute to milder fibroid symptoms in some patients.

Key takeaways:

Wegovy is not a fibroid treatment.

Indirect benefits are possible through weight reduction.

Established fibroid treatments (hormonal therapy, uterine artery embolization, myomectomy, hysterectomy) remain the standard of care.

Because fibroid symptoms — heavy bleeding, pelvic pain, bloating, and pressure — overlap with many other conditions, self-diagnosing can delay effective care. Uterine fibroids are non-cancerous growths in or on the uterus that affect up to 70% of women by menopause. They can cause heavy bleeding, pelvic pain, bloating and pressure. Recently, glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists like Wegovy (semaglutide) have gained popularity for weight loss. This raises the question: can Wegovy shrink fibroids or relieve fibroid-related pain?

Below, we'll explore what science and clinical experience tell us about the connection between Wegovy and fibroids.

Is There a Direct Link Between Wegovy and Fibroid Shrinkage?

Short answer: No clinical trials have demonstrated that semaglutide (Wegovy) directly shrinks fibroids. Wegovy's primary effects are on appetite, weight loss and metabolic parameters—not on fibroid tissue itself.

Why There's No Direct Evidence

Wegovy targets the GLP-1 receptor to control appetite and blood sugar, not the hormone receptors on fibroid cells (estrogen/progesterone).

No published randomized controlled trials or case series specifically examine semaglutide's impact on fibroid volume.

Fibroid size is driven by sex hormones and growth factors, not by the metabolic pathways modulated by GLP-1 receptor agonists.

Potential Indirect Benefits of Weight Loss on Fibroids

Even though Wegovy does not directly attack fibroids, losing excess weight can influence fibroid symptoms:

Adipose tissue (fat) produces estrogen. Less fat often means slightly lower estrogen levels, which could slow fibroid growth.

Obesity is linked with chronic low-grade inflammation. Weight loss can reduce pro-inflammatory cytokines that may contribute to fibroid growth.

Shedding pounds can ease pelvic pressure and improve blood flow to pelvic organs, potentially relieving pain and discomfort.

Weight reduction often leads to better blood pressure, fewer metabolic comorbidities and improved self-esteem—all factors that can indirectly improve quality of life for those with fibroids.

While Wegovy does not directly reduce cramps, it may help in these ways:

Weight loss can decrease abdominal girth, taking pressure off the pelvis.

Many patients who lose weight become more active, strengthening core muscles that support the pelvis.

If a modest drop in estrogen occurs with weight loss, some women notice lighter periods, potentially easing cramping.

Enhanced mood and energy from weight loss can improve coping with chronic discomfort.

It's important to note that individual experiences vary widely. If pelvic pain or heavy bleeding persists, specialized fibroid treatments may be more effective.

Established Treatments for Fibroids

If you're seeking direct fibroid relief, talk to your doctor about these options:

Gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonists or antagonists (temporary shrinkage)

Selective progesterone receptor modulators (e.g., ulipristal in some countries)

Tranexamic acid and NSAIDs for bleeding and pain

Uterine artery embolization (cuts off fibroid blood supply)

MRI-guided focused ultrasound (thermal ablation)

Myomectomy (removal of fibroids, preserving the uterus)

Hysterectomy (complete removal of the uterus)

Regular low-impact exercise

Anti-inflammatory diet rich in fruits, vegetables and whole grains

Stress management techniques like yoga and meditation
