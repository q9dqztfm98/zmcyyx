About the Symptom

Nipples that are inverted or retracted point inward toward the breast or lay flat instead of pointing outward. This condition is very common and usually is a very benign (not cancer) change that is just the way some people are born. ANY nipple change in an adult woman should prompt a visit to the doctor, Particularly if the nipple inverts (points in) over a short period of time.
