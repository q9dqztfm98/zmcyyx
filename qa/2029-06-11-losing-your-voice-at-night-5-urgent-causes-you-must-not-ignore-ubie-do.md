What factors contribute to hoarseness in the evening?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Evening hoarseness is often caused by laryngopharyngeal reflux (LPR), a condition where stomach acid travels up the esophagus and irritates the larynx and throat tissues. Symptoms typically worsen after meals or when lying down, and may include a raspy voice, throat clearing, chronic cough, or the sensation of a lump in the throat. Other common causes include vocal strain, allergies, dehydration, or postnasal drip.

Because hoarseness can stem from many overlapping conditions, identifying the true cause matters for choosing the right treatment.
