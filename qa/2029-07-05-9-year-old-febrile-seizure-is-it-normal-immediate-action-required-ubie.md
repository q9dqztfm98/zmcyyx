Is it normal for a 9-year-old to have a febrile seizure?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

[Normal Immediate](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

Internal Medicine

Febrile seizures are uncommon in a 9-year-old. These fever-related seizures most often occur in children between 6 months and 5 years old, with peak incidence around 12 to 18 months of age. By age 6, most children have outgrown the risk. When seizures occur alongside a fever in an older child, it may point to another underlying cause that warrants medical evaluation rather than a typical febrile seizure.

Explanation

Febrile seizures are convulsions brought on by a fever in young children. These seizures are most commonly seen in children between the ages of 6 months and 5 years.

A febrile seizure in a 9-year-old is not typical, as most children outgrow the risk by that age. Febrile seizures may occur after 5 years, but it is not the norm.
