# Want to Live in the Healthiest City in the Nation?

Do you live in one of the healthiest cities in the U.S.? If you live in Naples, Florida, Barnstable Town, Massachusetts, or Santa Cruz-Watsonville, California, then congrats, you do! At least, according to the Gallup-Healthways 2016 Community Well-Being Index.

To put together the index, researchers examined 350,000 interviews between 2015 and 2016 to rank the top 189 communities by emotional, physical, financial, and social health. For the second year in a row, Naples topped the list, with Barnstable Town and Santa Cruz coming in second and third, respectively.

"Naples is a high well-being place," Dan Witters, index research director, told the Today Show in an interview in 2016. "People in Naples really like their communities."
