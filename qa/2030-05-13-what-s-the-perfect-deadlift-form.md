# What's the Perfect Deadlift Form?

Q: Hey Muscle Guy! What's the perfect deadlifting form? I want to nail a P.R.! -Phillip S. Hailey, ID

A: So you want to pull a personal record deadlift? That’s an honorable goal, but to do that you're going to have to nail down your form first. Unfortunately, in deadlifting, there's no tried and true form that works perfectly for everyone. That’s because a person’s body type—things like their arm and torso length—determine the form that they should use while deadlifting.
